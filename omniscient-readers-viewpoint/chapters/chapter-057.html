Glorious Return

[Exclusive skill, ‘Fourth Wall’, is activating strongly!]

The moment ‘Secretive Plotter’ stared at me, [Fourth Wall] stirred.

[Fourth Wall], growling like a wild beast protecting its young.

「Be care ful Kim Dok ja」

When I first came here, ‘Secretive Plotter’ had called [Fourth Wall] the ‘Final Wall’. The original novel had never given any information about that, but it wasn't as though I had no guesses at all. There were several kinds of walls in ‘Ways of Survival’, including Jang Hayoung's ‘Unidentifiable Wall’.

“I've come to receive my rewards, Secretive Plotter.”

Despite my words, ‘Secretive Plotter’ gave no answer.

Tzzt, tzzzt……!

Sparks flew around ‘Secretive Plotter’. The pitch-black darkness filling their face in place of an expression was questioning me, as though angry.

【Demon King of Salvation, why did you make that choice?】

“Pardon?”

Asked like that out of nowhere, I had no way of knowing what it meant.

‘Secretive Plotter’ asked again.

【Why did you not want to return to the third round? That worldline wasn't even your round.】

“That world once saved me. That's all.”

【Had you left him alone, he could have found rest.】

“That isn't rest. ……Secretive Plotter, why did you send me to that worldline?”

Tzzzzzt……

The sparks around ‘Secretive Plotter’ grew fiercer.

As though their very existence had become unstable.

With a sigh, ‘Secretive Plotter’ spoke.

【Everything was part of a story whose course was already set…… You don't know what you have changed.】

The pure-white holes where eyes should have been were looking at me.

【I will give you the promised rewards.】

I nodded.

There were three rewards promised for this sub-scenario.

An item, a Story, and a skill obtainable in the 1863rd round.

【Which item will you take?】

“This coat.”

A little tense, I held out the white coat Han Sooyoung had given me. The eyes of ‘Secretive Plotter’ narrowed, light seeping from them.

As expected, deceiving an ‘Outer God’ seemed to have been too much to hope for.

‘Secretive Plotter’ had promised one item.

But there was at least one item inside this coat's inner pockets.

【……Very well. There is one less flower returning, after all. The total probability should be about the same.】

[You receive ‘Infinite Dimension Space Coat’ as your reward item.]

Fortunately, ‘Secretive Plotter’ turned a blind eye to Han Sooyoung's coat.

Next came the ‘Story’.

【Naturally, the Story you will take is ‘that one’?】

“That's right.”

The only Story I had obtained in the 1863rd round was the one Yoo Joonghyuk had given me, anyway.

In fact, acquiring that Story here would mean revising part of my planned course…… But it didn't matter.

What mattered was seeing the end of the story I had chosen.

[You have received your reward Story.]

[This Story will take effect normally after you return to your original worldline.]

The last reward was a ‘skill’.

【What will you do about the skill? You wouldn't have acquired any new ones.】

“I didn't acquire a skill, but…… Would it be possible to receive the reward in this form?”

I began explaining to ‘Secretive Plotter’.

After listening to me for a while, ‘Secretive Plotter’ nodded.

【It is possible.】

[You have received your reward skill.]

[This skill will take effect normally after you return to your original worldline.]

That was everything I needed to obtain.

Something unexpected happened just as I finished checking everything and was about to ask ‘Secretive Plotter’ to send me back to my worldline.

Tzzzzzzzt……!

Amid powerful sparks, someone was trying to break into this space from beyond it.

I recognized the kind of ‘Status’ I could feel from the other side. Apparently, the <Bureau> had discovered that ‘Secretive Plotter’ had orchestrated this affair.

In other words, the one trying to intrude was most likely one of the Bureau's great dokkaebis.

【Go, Demon King of Salvation.】

With a slight movement of ‘Secretive Plotter's’ fingertips, a swirling portal appeared beneath my feet.

I urgently asked ‘Secretive Plotter’ a question.

“Wait! How many years have passed in the worldline I'm returning to?”

At my words, ‘Secretive Plotter’ looked back at me.

【Some stories are read quickly, and some are read slowly.】

At the bleakness in that voice, I tried to speak. But the lower part of my face was already passing through the portal.

【Demon King of Salvation. Do you think a story from which you have vanished would be read quickly, or slowly?】

From somewhere, I heard what sounded like playful laughter.

The portal activated fully, and my vision soon warped beyond recognition.

【When we meet again, I hope you will have become the proper owner of that ‘wall’.】

[‘Viceroy's Dimensional Door’ is setting space-time coordinates.]

The passing scenery of the <Star Stream>.

A spacious room, with dim lamps hanging from the ceiling. I thought I could hear murmuring from somewhere.

I didn't seem to be on Earth yet.

Why? Why had I suddenly come here?

[The <Star Stream> has detected your existence in the worldline!]

[The <Star Stream> has recognized your modifier again.]

[The <Star Stream> assigns you a constellation context.]

[The <Bureau> assesses your Status.]

I thought I understood what had happened.

Through the ‘Otherworld Pledge’, I had left the scenarios of my original worldline.

There was a price to pay to reenter my original scenario.

Tzzzzzt……

The messages continued to appear as every muscle in my body seemed to spasm.

[The Bureau has completed your assessment.]

[You are unsuited to returning to your original scenario.]

[The Bureau is reviewing scenarios appropriate to your level.]

[The review is complete.]

I sighed at the messages that followed.

……I had thought it might turn out this way.

No, in truth, it had been inevitable.

I had left the worldline where I originally lived and visited another world.

And although the categories differed, beings like me who strayed far from their original scenarios and came back were all given the same name.

[You have acquired a new Story!]

[You have acquired a new attribute!]

[You have gained the qualifications of a ‘returnee’.]

[A new scenario is being prepared for you.]

They were all returnees like me.

“Hahaha, I can finally go back to Earth! It's been ten years!”

“It's been twenty years for yours truly.”

“Where did you go? From your clothes, I take it you're from Murim?”

“I came from ‘Gratus’. Ever heard of it?”

That was when someone waved at me.

“Oh, looks like you're the last one, friend.”

It was a man in black martial robes. He wore a mask, but something about his exposed eyes and expression looked familiar.

I gave him a brief greeting, and he spoke to me again.

“Where are you from, friend?”

“Earth.”

“No, that's not what I meant. Is there anyone here who isn't from Earth……? I was asking which planet you were transported to.”

The man, smiling as he spoke in an easygoing tone. Suddenly, I thought I knew who he was.

「Cheeks bulging plumply like bags of tricks. Narrow eyes suited to night vision, and eyebrows half gone, as though pecked away by a crow.」

I inwardly gave a hollow laugh.

To think it was already time for this man to appear……

“The place I visited was Earth too. Earth in another parallel dimension.”

“Hmm, is that so? What an unusual fellow you are.”

The man held out a hand to me as he spoke.

“Let me introduce myself first. My name is Wang Weirong. In the <Second Murim>, where I've been, I'm known by the alias ‘Flying Fox’.”

Flying Fox (飛天狐狸), Wang Weirong (王卫荣).

He was one of the people who would later become Yoo Joonghyuk's companions.

“My name is Kim Dokja. As for an alias…… Hmm…… Shall we say ‘Demon King of Salvation’?”

Several returnees listening nearby burst into laughter.

“Demon King of Salvation? Bwahahahaha……!”

“That's quite a grand title! You must have been to some third-rate fantasy world!”

Hands slapping my back. Most of the returnees were from Murim or medieval-style other worlds.

Flying Fox grinned and spoke.

“A fine alias, friend.”

“You're too kind.”

“But it seems you've already been issued ‘returnee clothing’.”

“Ah, yes.”

I looked down at the [Infinite Dimension Space Coat] I was wearing. I'd completely forgotten that these clothes had originally been made exclusively for returnees.

I remembered Myung Ilsang, the ‘Disaster of Questions’ I had once fought. He had probably been summoned to Earth through the same procedure.

Just then, dokkaebis appeared in the air and began distributing coats to the returnees. Several who had received theirs looked back and forth between their coats and mine, then protested.

“Hey, dokkaebi! That fellow's coat looks better than mine……!”

Of course it did. It would make no sense for a coat from as far along as Scenario 95 to be inferior to a standard-issue one.

I saw several dokkaebis spot me, start in surprise, and report something to their superiors.

Would Bihyung know I'd come back by now?

I wanted to see my companions on Earth soon.

How many years had passed?

I hoped everyone was doing well.

Having received his returnee coat, Flying Fox grinned broadly and spoke.

“Ahh, the clothes really make the man. What will you do first when you get back, friend?”

“I'm going to look for someone first.”

“Oh, have you got a sweetheart waiting for you?”

I smiled. Flying Fox continued.

“Once I get back, I'm going to live it up. I've gained all this power, after all. No more cowering before that jerk who used to throw his weight around! The hard times are over. Nothing but happiness from here!”

“……It won't all be pleasant. Earth will have changed a great deal too.”

“Now, now, friend. You're sounding defeatist already. After everything we went through in other worlds, what couldn't we do back on Earth?”

[Returnees, please give us your attention.]

When the commotion subsided, the intermediate dokkaebi responsible for the returnees spoke on behalf of the others.

[Your return to Earth will begin shortly. You will be assigned a ‘main scenario exclusively for returnees’, which you will carry out in your homeland. We hope you will think of it as a simple game and enjoy yourselves.]

“A returnee scenario? Tell us exactly what it is!”

[Please check the scenario window for details. Ah, for reference, this is a group scenario, so there will be a leader. The being with the highest Status among you will automatically be selected as leader. Please bear that in mind.]

“Naturally, the leader will be me!”

“That Murim fellow over there looks pretty strong……”

They seemed curious about who would be this group's ‘king’.

[Your return to Earth begins!]

With a dazzling flash of light, all ten returnees began teleporting at once.

My vision shimmered briefly. When I opened my eyes again, we had landed in the middle of a sprawling city.

Broken paving stones, with gaps patched here and there.

A skyline formed by buildings whose reconstruction had begun.

It was unmistakably Earth.

“Where are we? Those letters……”

“It's Korea! Korea!”

A returnee from the Korean Peninsula jumped about in excitement.

Recalling the information about the ‘returnee scenario’, I shouted.

“Wait. Everyone, calm down and gather here for a moment.”

The returnees were already beyond control.

Several impatient returnees grabbed passing civilians and asked them questions.

“Excuse me, where are we? And what year AD is it?”

I sighed.

[The ‘Returnee Trigger’ has been activated!]

“They asked the d-date! They're returnees!”

“Aaaagh! Returnees! Everyone, run!”

“Report it to the alliance! Hurry!”

“What? Did I do something wrong?”

[Main Scenario #45 ― ‘Glorious Return (錦衣還鄕)’ begins!]

With a burst of sparks, my appearance was changing.

The form of a monster, hideous to anyone who saw it, seething with tentacles and filth.

Though that wasn't what I actually looked like, outwardly I resembled a small Outer God.

I probably looked like that to everyone on Earth too.

Because to the people of Earth, every returnee was a disaster (災殃).

Earth's incarnations, approaching step by step with hostility in their eyes.

When I looked back, the dazed Flying Fox was parting his trembling lips.

“F-friend. What on earth is……”

“Didn't I tell you it wouldn't all be pleasant?”

[You are the ‘King of the 163rd Returnee Group’.]

Acrid smoke spreading through the hazy field of vision.

What the hell was this?

Krrrrang!

……Yoo Joonghyuk?

What on earth was happening? What on earth was she seeing?

Kim Dokja, on his knees like a man who had lost everything. Just as Han Sooyoung reached out and approached, someone called to her from right beside her.

—That's far enough.

Han Sooyoung bit her lip hard.

If only he had been here……

It had also been a long time since their hope that he would return had begun to fade.

“Sitting here after so long reminds me of when I worked at the company. Back then, I'd slip into the break room to hide like this whenever I had a spare moment.”

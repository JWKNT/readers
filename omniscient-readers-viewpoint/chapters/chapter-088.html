Myth-grade Constellation

Uriel was not in a very good mood.

―Where’s our Biyoo?

The past Stories of <Kim Dokja’s Company> were playing on the Star Stream broadcast, but she could hardly take them in. It was only natural.

So many things had happened at once lately that even the optimistic Uriel had a lot on her mind.

The decisive moment, in particular, had been meeting her other self from the 999th-round worldline and receiving some of her memories.

「I am your only companion, Yoo Joonghyuk. I will end the scenarios and avenge you, without fail.」

She had already known she existed in other worldlines. But she had never imagined how different knowing it would be from seeing it for herself.

The 999th-round worldline.

Just what had happened to her there?

[……Ah, this is so annoying. Other worldlines aren’t what I want to know about. I can barely keep up with our kids’ Stories as it is.]

Uriel grumbled, clutching her head.

The atmosphere in the <Star Stream> had already been unsettling lately. As the Final Scenario drew nearer, a strange tension had begun to spread among the constellations.

There were even rumors that the Bureau had abandoned this worldline.

[The <Bureau> summons the constellation ‘Demon-like Judge of Fire’!]

[Will you accept the summons?]

Uriel’s head shot up at the sudden message.

……Why now, of all times?

After a moment’s thought, Uriel pressed the confirmation button. A dazzling light enveloped her, and her body was transported somewhere.

[Transfer complete.]

She had been transported to an unfamiliar clearing.

Several constellations, including her, had already arrived there.

[What? Gabriel, you’re here too?]

[If you turn down things like this, the Bureau keeps pestering you with spam.]

Looking around, she saw that dozens of constellations had already gathered.

Most, like Uriel, were wondering what was going on. Thuggish as the Bureau was, it wouldn’t summon them for no reason.

There were even some familiar faces among them. Short, with bandages wrapped tightly around one arm…….

[Aww, if it isn’t our little Flame Dragon!]

Uriel dashed over and caught Abyssal Black Flame Dragon in a headlock.

[Kh! Is this an enemy ambush?!]

[It’s me, it’s me. Your archangel noona.]

[Let go!]

Abyssal Black Flame Dragon flailed in Uriel’s arms, aghast.

Watching them, Gabriel muttered,

[……Uriel, he’s ‘Absolute Evil.’]

[Who cares? <Eden> is ruined now anyway. We should all get along.]

The nebula <Eden> had been all but annihilated in the last ‘Great War of Saints and Demons.’ Most of its mighty archangel army had been wiped out, and Uriel and Gabriel were the only archangels still able to act.

Setting aside her bitterness, Uriel looked around again.

[Isn’t that Goryeo’s First Sword?]

The constellations of the Korean Peninsula were there, including ‘Bald General of Justice,’ ‘First Spiritualist of Joseon,’ and ‘The Last Hero of Hwangsanbeol.’ She could also see Surya and constellations from other nebulas, including <Olympus>.

Uriel’s eyes darted about more rapidly.

The more familiar faces she saw, the worse her misgivings grew.

All the constellations gathered here had one thing in common.

Having barely escaped Uriel, Black Flame Dragon muttered,

[……They were all in Kim Dokja’s channel.]

He was right. Everyone gathered here was―

……Bzzzt.

Just then, Uriel sensed a menacing Status stirring.

Beings were surrounding the clearing. Each with Status nearly as powerful as that of a Narrative-grade constellation.

Quick-witted as she was, Uriel soon realized who they were.

[……<Papyrus>, <Vedas>, and <Emperor>. What are you thinking? You don’t even get along, and now you’re joining forces?]

Uriel muttered, slightly tense. Even she could not fight so many Narrative-grade constellations. And on top of that―

[Even an old man who never gets off his backside……. What brings a being of the ‘Final Scenario’ here?]

There was no doubt. The goosebumps that had been prickling her forearms were proof of an immensely powerful being somewhere nearby.

Vast as the <Star Stream> was, only a handful of beings possessed Status of this level. Beside her, ‘Abyssal Black Flame Dragon’ had visibly stiffened too.

There was no doubt. This being was

[Has everyone gathered?]

A complete Myth-grade constellation.

Bzzzzzzzzzt!

The moment the True Voice sounded, the air around them changed completely. It felt as though every bit of oxygen drifting through the air had caught fire.

The surrounding constellations staggered. Even Uriel, with her high resistance to fire, winced for a moment.

……What was a ‘Myth-grade constellation’ doing here?

Apart from rare exceptions such as the King of the Underworld, Metatron, and the Great Sage, Heaven’s Equal, most ‘Myth-grade constellations’ did not interfere in lower scenarios.

They had already completed their own ‘Conclusion’ and become candidates for ‘A Single Story.’

Those who had reached the Final Scenario and secured their Stories.

[The monkey and the King of the Underworld haven’t come. But we cannot delay any longer, so I will begin.]

[Wait!]

[The constellation ‘Midday Sun’ gazes at ‘Demon-like Judge of Fire.’]

The moment their eyes met, Uriel realized who that True Voice belonged to.

There were countless ‘suns’ in the <Star Stream>.

But very few of them occupied the center of this universe.

Especially a being who occupied ‘midday,’ the center of time―

[The sun god Ra. Did you summon us?]

Ra. He was the highest-ranking constellation of the great nebula <Papyrus>.

[Yes.]

[That’s strange. I thought it was the ‘Bureau’ that summoned us?]

Ra did not answer.

Among the faint presences she could sense around her was that of a Great Dokkaebi.

Uriel decided to remain calm. If they really had colluded with the Bureau at the cost of probability, things were very bad indeed.

[All right, let’s hear what’s so important that you’re willing to expose your collusion with the Bureau.]

[I summoned you because of the ‘Final Scenario.’ ‘A Single Story’ will soon be chosen. One story to represent this worldline will be decided.]

A Single Story.

Not a constellation here was unaware of it.

After all, <Kim Dokja’s Company>, which they had watched, was also a nebula creating ‘A Single Story.’

[And? What does that have to do with us?]

[Most of you have not qualified for the ‘Final Scenario.’ But with me, it will be different. I will take you to the ‘Final Scenario’ with me. I am offering you a chance to put your modifiers on ‘A Single Story.’]

The gazes of some constellations wavered at the offer.

They were mostly Historical-grade constellations without affiliations.

Uriel watched Ra in silence, then gave a short laugh.

[What? Is that all? No thanks. If you’re done talking, I’m leaving.]

But when she turned, she could not take a step.

Something—an immensely powerful Status—had seized her ankles.

[……What are you doing?]

[I am not finished.]

[I think I know without hearing the rest.]

There was a sharp edge to Uriel’s True Voice.

Of all channels, they had summoned only the constellations of channel BY-9158.

And the one who had summoned them was none other than <Papyrus>’s highest-ranking constellation.

[You’re about to suggest we attack <Kim Dokja’s Company>.]

A very brief silence followed. Ra asked,

[……What makes you think that?]

[Because they’ve qualified for the ‘Final Scenario.’ And if you get rid of them, that naturally removes one of the leading candidates for ‘A Single Story.’]

An uproar spread among the constellations. She could feel the Status of those surrounding them waver. Uriel snorted.

[Constellations really never change, right to the end. And Ra, you’ve already reached the Final Scenario. Stop meddling in lower scenarios. You and Zeus both…….]

[…….]

[Are you worried about your children? Are you angry that the children who inherited your Stories couldn’t handle a mere fledgling nebula and failed to become candidates for ‘A Single Story’―]

With an immense explosion, Uriel’s body was driven deep into the ground.

As Uriel swore, Ra’s True Voice reached her.

[■ck, what the…….]

[Yes, you are right. This is the anger of a parent who has raised his children poorly. The rightful anger of a parent who cannot watch his children’s future be ruined by pathetic losers who have given up on the finale and a brat of a nebula not even ten years old.]

Uriel shouted back as though she had known it all along.

[So you’re finally showing your true colors. Sorry, but no constellation here will take your children’s side. You must not know whose channel we subscribe to.]

Black Flame Dragon and Gabriel reached out and pulled Uriel from the pit.

Behind them, Goryeo’s First Sword, Bald General of Justice, and the other constellations of the Korean Peninsula were nodding.

Meeting their eyes, Uriel felt an inexplicable pride.

Gathered here were beings united in their support for someone’s Story.

From the first scenario, ‘Proof of Qualification,’ all the way to the ‘Final Scenario’ that would decide the worldline’s fate.

She could not know whether everyone here felt the same way she did. But surely some of them loved the Story of <Kim Dokja’s Company> more than their own. Just as she did.

[That is why you are losers.]

[What?]

[Have you been so absorbed in your voyeurism that you’ve forgotten you, too, are part of the scenarios?]

The next moment, someone appeared in midair amid a violent storm of probability.

Bzzzzzt!

A doll with a steel exterior. A Narrative-grade constellation covered in wounds was trapped within a solid ring of light.

[This fool supported a foolish Story, just like you.]

Uriel stared blankly at the constellation.

A constellation she had never met in person. Yet the moment she saw him, she felt she knew who he was.

She had even exchanged indirect messages with him a few times.

[The constellation ‘Master of Steel’ recoils in pain.]

Master of Steel.

He was the constellation sponsor of the incarnation ‘Lee Hyunsung.’

Ra’s laughter rang out.

[He would have been safe if he had stayed quietly in <Oz>, but to help a foolish Story, he contacted a being from another worldline.]

[What are you―!]

[You have no choice. Either help us put an end to the Story of <Kim Dokja’s Company>, or…….]

As he spoke, the ring of light constricting Master of Steel’s entire body began to shrink. Writhing in agony, Master of Steel looked at Uriel.

[The constellation ‘Master of Steel’ says there is no point in killing him.]

[The constellation ‘Master of Steel’ cries out that he has already passed his Stories and modifier on to another being!]

The ring was shrinking further and further. Uriel moved.

And

[The constellation ‘Master of Steel’ asks you not to give up on the story.]

Crrrunch!

Stories burst from Master of Steel’s limp, crumpled body.

The futile death of a Narrative-grade constellation.

Everyone in the channel stared at the sight as though frozen.

In the face of a star’s death, Ra spoke.

[Or die like this.]

With a frenzied cry, Uriel released her Status.

Rrrrrumble!

Black cracks split the sky like shattered glass.

Lee Hyunsung looked up at the cracks spreading across the sky and asked,

“Sergeant Kim Dokja-nim, is this really all right?”

“…….”

At that, I looked up at the sky too. The world was falling apart.

The reason was clear. Someone was attacking <Oz> from outside.

And immensely powerful beings at that.

When I turned, my companions were looking at me too. Yoo Joonghyuk, Han Sooyoung, Yoo Sangah, Jung Heewon…….

Our decision was already made. We didn’t need to say it.

“It’s fine. You think I’m a sergeant for nothing? Don’t you worry about a thing.”

●

The sky is breaking. How can he say something like this is all right?

Lee Hyunsung could not understand it. Was this what the army was supposed to be like?

「Kim Dokja simply smiled quietly.」

A smile that rose like a neatly composed sentence.

Sergeant Kim Dokja only said,

“The company commander will say it’s fine too.”

And indeed, not long afterward, the company commander gathered the soldiers on the parade ground and made a major announcement.

Charismatic despite her small stature, she surveyed the soldiers with her characteristic expression, then spoke.

“Your company commander is deeply disappointed in you.”

The soldiers tensed at the unexpected opening.

“You did not read web novels during your personal time.”

Lee Hyunsung flinched inwardly. It was true. Just yesterday, instead of reading web novels during his personal time, he had practiced the armed forces’ calisthenics with Yoo Joonghyuk.

“And so your company commander intends to leave this unit.”

Lee Hyunsung was dumbfounded by the unexpected declaration of desertion.

……Leave? Murmurs rose all around.

“And Lee Hyunsung.”

When he came to his senses, the company commander had a hand on his shoulder.

“Private Lee Hyunsung!”

He had never seen the company commander this close before.

Her rank and name were written on her immaculate uniform.

Captain Han Sooyoung. That was her rank and name.

「“How long are you going to stand there spacing out? Get moving! You want to watch Kim Dokja croak?”」

Why? A strange memory flashed through his mind with a sudden prickling sensation.

What was that? Just now…….

“There you go again, spacing out again.”

“P-Private Lee Hyunsung!”

The company commander watched him with an unreadable look, then patted his cheek and said,

“Take your reading seriously. You’re an idiot, so you need to read lots of books. That’s how you’ll live a long time.”

Leaving those incomprehensible words behind, Company Commander Han Sooyoung left the unit.

●

Two days passed after Company Commander Han Sooyoung left.

The cracks in the sky were still growing. It was like watching the signs of a world’s destruction.

“Lee Hyunsung. Have you memorized all the exercises?”

He turned to find his immediate senior, Private First Class Yoo Joonghyuk.

“Private Lee Hyunsung! I have memorized them perfectly!”

“And have you filled the water container in the barracks?”

“Exactly two liters!”

Before Yoo Joonghyuk’s fierce gaze, Lee Hyunsung felt needlessly cowed.

Even though he had done nothing wrong.

“What about the barracks code of conduct?”

“P-Private Lee Hyunsung! Not yet……!”

He regretted it the moment he spoke. He was in for another scolding. Lee Hyunsung swallowed and squeezed his eyes shut, just as Yoo Joonghyuk’s voice reached him.

“You’ll memorize it soon. It’s short.”

“……Pardon? Ah, did I mishear? I mean, I misheard!”

What was happening? Even though he had made two mistakes in a row, Yoo Joonghyuk did not reprimand him.

Even his gaze was no longer fierce.

“I’m transferring out tomorrow.”

“……I beg your pardon?”

“Lee Hyunsung, you can’t put everything in a manual. There won’t always be a senior to help you.”

Why?

Why did Private First Class Yoo Joonghyuk’s retreating back look so familiar?

“Sometimes you have to choose even without a manual.”

Those were the last words Private First Class Yoo Joonghyuk left him.

●

The company’s personnel were disappearing one by one. Company Commander Han Sooyoung, Private First Class Yoo Joonghyuk, and First Lieutenant Yoo Sangah vanished in turn. Before he knew it, the highest-ranking commander left was the noncommissioned officer, Sergeant First Class Jung Heewon. (It made no sense, but Lee Hyunsung decided that such things could happen in an emergency.)

Each day, after morning and evening roll call, Lee Hyunsung maintained the unit’s facilities with Sergeant First Class Jung Heewon or visited the barracks library with Sergeant Kim Dokja.

“They have martial arts novels in the army these days too. Wow, this book is really old.”

Kim Dokja liked books. In fact, he did more than like them. He was the kind of person who did nothing but read all day.

Lee Hyunsung would sit beside him and quietly watch him eagerly turn the pages.

“Want to read it too?”

“Uh, I, I…….”

Before he could finish answering, another deafening roar echoed through the sky.

Kim Dokja’s expression stiffened slightly.

When that roar first sounded four days ago, Captain Han Sooyoung had disappeared. When it sounded a second time two days ago, Private First Class Yoo Joonghyuk had disappeared.

Lee Hyunsung grew anxious.

“Sergeant Kim Dokja-nim.”

“Yeah.”

“Will you be leaving too, Sergeant Kim Dokja-nim?”

People leave him.

He keeps losing things.

Kim Dokja smiled at Lee Hyunsung.

“Probably. I’m a sergeant, after all. I need to get discharged soon. I have absolutely no intention of staying in for good.”

“……Yes, sir.”

“You want to get out soon too, don’t you?”

Lee Hyunsung was about to say, I want to leave―when the barbed wire outside the window suddenly caught his eye. It looked very strong, and very dangerous.

For some reason, he was now afraid to go beyond it.

“I…….”

If he tried to climb over it carelessly, he would surely be hurt. But as long as he stayed inside, that barbed wire would be a shield protecting him.

The thought put Lee Hyunsung at ease.

The crumbling sky. Out there lay a world he did not know.

A world where the barracks code of conduct and the armed forces’ calisthenics meant nothing.

When he looked up, Kim Dokja was watching him. Kim Dokja’s lips moved as though he would say something, then he gave another easy smile.

“If you want to leave, read. Read books.”

“……Does reading a lot of books shorten your term of service?”

At that, Kim Dokja stuck out his lips and said,

“If you read a book and write a report, you can win some leave, at least.”

A book report?

“The division’s holding a book report contest. Read that and enter. If you win, you get reward leave.”

A poster for the military contest was pinned to the noticeboard Kim Dokja pointed at. Lee Hyunsung had never known there was such a thing.

I see. A book report. If I write one of those, I can go on reward leave.

“When you finish it, make sure you read it to me too.”

Sergeant Kim Dokja disappeared just after morning roll call the next day.

●

“What duties? There are only two of us left.”

Sergeant First Class Jung Heewon’s grumbling voice.

Lee Hyunsung smiled awkwardly as he pulled weeds around the base.

“You never know. The company commander might come back…….”

Sitting on a bench with her chin in her hand, Jung Heewon watched Lee Hyunsung as though observing a curious animal.

“Do you like it here?”

It was a tone Sergeant First Class Jung Heewon would not normally use. And yet it stirred a strange longing in Lee Hyunsung.

Perhaps that longing was what allowed him to be honest at once.

“I neither like it nor dislike it.”

A place he neither liked nor disliked.

That was precisely how Lee Hyunsung felt about the ‘army.’

“It’s just that, here, I don’t have to think about anything.”

That was right.

That was why he had chosen the army.

While he was here, he could forget the world. Finding a job, his studies, other people’s scrutiny, worldly problems, family matters—even worries he could never resolve, no matter what he did.

“……But lately, to be honest, I thought I liked it a little.”

What was it he liked? He could not express it well.

「“I like you.”」

Why did his chest hurt so much?

Facing him, Sergeant First Class Jung Heewon said,

“Then stay here, Lee Hyunsung. Wait here until we come back.”

He could not say, I beg your pardon.

Because he could not possibly have misheard.

“We’ll protect your world.”

Just as he was about to say something, dazzling light poured from the air.

And Sergeant First Class Jung Heewon disappeared before his eyes.

Rrrrrumble.

Before he knew it, the cracks had swallowed half the sky.

And so Lee Hyunsung was left alone.

●

What am I doing?

Is this really the army?

The army I know…….

Guarding the empty base, Lee Hyunsung repeated his routine. He woke at the appointed time, ran, and did the armed forces’ calisthenics. After completing his ideological training alone, he began his daily duties.

But there was nothing left to do. He had finished pulling all the weeds on the base yesterday.

“···A book report.”

Belatedly, Lee Hyunsung remembered Kim Dokja’s words.

He had told him to write a book report. To read a book, and write a report.

Lee Hyunsung went up to the barracks library. A pile of books remained, telling him Kim Dokja had once been there.

Amid the echo of a strange emotion, Lee Hyunsung picked up the book on top. It was familiar.

『The Wizard of Oz. ver 999』

A book whose title he had heard before.

But one he had never properly read.

For now, Lee Hyunsung opened the book and read the first sentence.

「The Tin Soldier was afraid of having feelings.」

The Tin Soldier. He must be the protagonist of The Wizard of Oz.

Lee Hyunsung kept turning the pages.

「The first companion the Tin Soldier met was a very frightening man. The Tin Soldier called him ‘Captain.’」

The moment he read that sentence, his head throbbed. Captain?

「The Tin Soldier became companions with a beautiful angel. When she was angry, the angel would sometimes turn into a demon.」

For some reason, reading that sentence made his chest ache.

「The Tin Soldier became companions with a warrior in thick armor. The warrior would often test the Tin Soldier’s hardness with their own sword.」

Why did it feel as though he could picture them before his eyes?

「The Tin Soldier welcomed a dragon that breathed terrifying fire as a companion. Sometimes, the dragon was like a little nuisance.」

I’ve never met beings like these.

「And a Demon King from another world took away what they cherished.」

With every sentence he read, scenes of pandemonium and screams flashed through his mind. He could not understand what he was seeing. And yet his whole body trembled.

I don’t know. What this story is.

I have no idea what the author wants to say.

And I don’t know why I feel like crying, either.

「At the end of the story, the Tin Soldier realized that he was hurting inside.」

「And that pain became his heart.」

As he recalled that sentence, Lee Hyunsung remembered.

I had comrades like these too.

「“After all this tragedy is over, when our story is no longer a scenario, I’d really like to hear your story, Hyunsung-ssi.”」

The first comrade was kind and warm. Everyone followed him.

「“The most important thing is that no one gets hurt before then.”」

The second comrade was caring. Everyone believed she was right.

「“No, it’s more important for everyone to survive, even if one person dies. Of course, that ‘one person’ has to be Kim Dokja. That bastard will come back to life somehow anyway.”」

The third comrade was clever. Everyone believed the plans she devised would succeed.

「“No one dies. Leave this to me and go.”」

The fourth comrade was strong. Everyone could trust him to watch their backs.

「“You know, Hyunsung-ssi. If I forget you.”」

And the fifth comrade was…….

「“Kill me.”」

His memories were returning. Very slowly, his heart was beating. With a slow but unmistakable rhythm, it gave its all to each beat, as if insisting: I am in such pain; such pain exists there.

How could he forget these people?

Lee Hyunsung’s body trembled as he clenched his fists. This was no time to stay here.

He looked at the sky outside the window. The cracks now covered the entire sky. It was clear where his companions had gone.

They had gone to protect this world where he existed.

To face a disaster that could not even be compared to North Korea.

「Lee Hyunsung thought, ‘Do I have that kind of strength too?’」

[The constellation ‘Master of Steel’ looks at you.]

His constellation sponsor was watching him.

Bzzt, bzzzt.

But something was different from usual.

It was certainly his sponsor, yet the gaze was subtly different from the one he had felt until now.

[The constellation ‘Master of Steel’ asks whether it hurts.]

Lee Hyunsung nodded.

「I want to protect these emotions, these feelings.」

He was afraid. That he would forget this moment again. That his heart would stop again. That everything would freeze in a cold silver light.

Then his constellation sponsor spoke.

【You can protect them.】

A voice like steel tempered for tens of thousands of years.

【But if you fail to protect them, you may spend eternity living in pain.】

“I don’t mind. It’s better than not even having the chance to protect them.”

He was already used to losing things.

What mattered was not losing them again.

【Your name is Steel Sword Emperor.】

In the distance, he saw the barbed wire collapse.

The world of manuals he had upheld was crumbling.

Lee Hyunsung walked out toward his own story.

“Dokja-ssi.”

We were defending the crumbling <Oz>.

As the Stories of Master of Steel and 『The Wizard of Oz』 declined, <Oz>’s air defenses were collapsing.

Hundreds of warships surrounded Oz. My companions paced themselves as they defended the planet riddled with holes.

But we were nearing our limit.

Their main attacks were long-range bombardments from their warships. Our only efficient means of countering them were Lee Jihye’s [Turtle Dragon] and Shin Yoosung’s [Chimera Dragon].

The problem was that neither Lee Jihye’s ship nor Shin Yoosung’s [Chimera Dragon] had been fully repaired since being damaged in the ‘Great War of Saints and Demons.’

We had come here to solve that very problem.

“The air defenses are about to fail!”

We prepared for the final clash.

Jung Heewon asked,

“Still no word from the other constellations?”

“It seems something’s happened.”

Perhaps it had something to do with those attacking us now.

Han Sooyoung grumbled,

“You really don’t regret this? Is it okay for us to do this?”

I nodded.

“Lee Hyunsung was always the one taking the sword blows on the front line. Now it’s our turn to repay him.”

The others agreed too.

Yoo Joonghyuk had already climbed the tallest building on the planet. Jung Heewon was steeling her resolve, radiating a Status mightier than anyone else’s.

「I trust Lee Hyunsung.」

I did not know how much time we could buy.

I could only hope it would be enough time for Lee Hyunsung.

“Here it comes!”

Boooooom!

In the distance, hundreds of warships fired at once. Enough magic shells to lay waste to the entire planet.

We all unfolded our Stories. Somehow, we had to withstand this blow.

Gathering all our Magic Power―and at that very moment.

A boundless silver light covered the world.

A vast barrier of Story metal spread across the sky. Beyond the translucent wall, the ships’ bombardment burst apart, neutralized.

“There was nothing happy about being left alone in a place like that.”

It was not the Story of 『The Wizard of Oz』.

A new kind of Story, somehow different at its very root.

「In that world, he was called Steel Sword Emperor.」

Boom, boom, boom!

Goosebumps ran down my back.

Steam covering the entire planet. Metal had grown across its surface like the spreading branches of an enormous tree.

The hardest substance in the <Star Stream>. The only Story metal capable of withstanding the weapons of those terrifying Myth-grade constellations.

[Many constellations are astonished by the scale of the Story!]

A stigma that could cover the entire planet in that metal.

This was [Last Steel], the air defense system the planet <Oz> prided itself on.

“Captain Lee Hyunsung, under the Anti-Monster Special Operations Command.”

Taller even than Yoo Joonghyuk.

The most solidly built man I knew.

“As of today, I have been ordered discharged.”

I saw the stars in the sky falter and draw back.

[The constellations of the nebula <Papyrus> are astonished that ‘Master of Steel’ has survived!]

At last, it was time to counterattack.

[You have become a Great Dokkaebi.]

[You may now participate in the Great Dokkaebis’ ‘Final Vote.’]

Reading the messages, Bihyung blinked slowly.

Bihyung’s appearance had changed greatly right after he met the Dokkaebi King. He now had a fully humanoid form, like Baram. The power of Stories radiating from the white tiger pelt over his shoulders and the three yellow horns that had sprouted.

He had now become a full-fledged ‘Great Dokkaebi.’

[I’ll be going now, Baram-nim.]

[You intend to go to them, I take it?]

Bihyung did not answer. Baram said,

[When the final hour comes, every dokkaebi must choose their last story. Even if telling it costs them their life.]

[…….]

[The ‘story’ you will choose has very little chance of victory.]

[I know.]

[They have even made enemies of the Bureau and many Great Dokkaebis.]

[I know that too.]

There was no dokkaebi in this <Star Stream> who did not know what it meant to become an enemy of the Bureau.

[Even so, I want to see the end of the world with that story.]

Bihyung chose his final Story.

“Dokja-ssi.”

“Are you here on reward leave?”

“You’re teasing me. I said I was discharged, not on leave.”

“Really discharged?”

“Yes.”

Lee Hyunsung smiled brightly.

“I’m not going to be a soldier anymore.”

I watched the silver Story flowing from his entire body.

Some novels give you a feel for them from the first paragraph. Stories are the same.

「A resolute silver glow. A steadfast will tempered over long years.」

On ‘Reincarnation Island,’ One Invincible Fist Yoo Hoseong had said that to command a Story, you first had to understand it. But just as it was impossible to understand a person completely, it was impossible to understand a Story.

「All we can do is offer our own interpretations.」

Then this must be the answer Lee Hyunsung had arrived at.

[The Story ‘Ruler of Steel’ begins its story.]

A core Story possessed by ‘Master of Steel.’ Finally being able to wield it meant that Lee Hyunsung had at last reached the final stage of [Steel Transformation].

“I couldn’t write the book report, but I understood why you told me to read the book, Dokja-ssi.”

Lee Hyunsung added cautiously,

“Was that perhaps the story of the 999th-round worldline?”

A man looking at his companions with sorrowful eyes.

But I couldn’t tell them now that it was actually me.

Plump, snow-white cheeks and clear eyes.

If I remembered correctly, Jang Hayoung was male.

Because that damned author of ‘Ways of Survival’ had accepted every one of my comments, then changed exactly one thing however they pleased.

And that was this kid’s gender.

“Trying to judge everything by appearances. Typical Earthling.”

Just as I was about to reply, the tavern owner suddenly turned off the lights.

Then he addressed the entire tavern in a very low voice.

“Night is coming.”

At those few quiet words, a deep silence spread through the tavern from the owner outward. A silence far more taut and sharp than when the word ‘Disaster’ had come up earlier. Meeting my eyes, Jang Hayoung put an index finger to their lips.

“Shh.”

Looking more closely, I saw it wasn’t just this tavern.

Other taverns and shops along the street were closing their doors and turning off their lights, too. Sounds were vanishing in an instant.

As though the entire [Industrial Complex] were sinking into the deep sea.

From the deserted street, something like the sound of an eerie flute began to drift in.

I saw several citizens cover their ears to shut out the sound.

At that moment, something came back to me, too.

「A special [Night] comes to the Demon Realm.」

Listening to the [Fourth Wall]’s voice, I recalled the setting of ‘Ways of Survival.’

「All citizens of the [Industrial Complex] fear the nobles. It wasn’t simply because the nobles were strong. It was because of this [Night], which came once every three days.」

“Please just pass us by. Please······.”

Someone murmured those words. How much time passed after that? With a crackling sound as the windows froze over, I sensed something passing along the street.

Sssssss.

Holding their breath, the citizens acted as though they couldn’t see it. Some even bowed their heads all the way down against the tables. The shadow of an enormous scythe, glimpsed now and then beyond the frozen windows.

「When Night falls, an [Executioner] appears in the Industrial Complex.」

「If the citizens have a [Revolutionary], the nobles have an [Executioner].」

The root of the citizens’ inability to resist the nobles despite their terror, and the reason the duke could maintain his position of absolute power in this Industrial Complex.

It all came down to the existence of that [Executioner].

Creeeeak.

A body that looked about twice the size of an adult man’s, with an appearance reminiscent of the Grim Reaper.

[The target is currently under the protection of the scenario.]

[The target is currently invincible.]

No being could stand against that [Executioner] inside the [Industrial Complex] at Night.

The owner who had served me food and the people who had noisily chatted while watching the scenario were all staring at the floor, their faces deathly pale.

Tonight, the [Executioner] had chosen this tavern as its execution ground.

Someone here would certainly die in this very place tonight.

[Who is the Re vo lu tion ar y.]

People curled in on themselves whenever the Executioner’s scythe swept past them. It looked almost like a game. As I studied the creature closely, Jang Hayoung tugged at my collar in alarm.

“Oh, fu······.”

More precisely, toward Jang Hayoung, who was swearing.

The face of someone sensing their own impending death.

「Kim Dokja thought. What would Yoo Joonghyuk have done?」

Yoo Joonghyuk would have kept himself hidden to the very end, looking for the situation that would benefit him most.

He would have completed every kind of investigation to enter the [Industrial Complex]’s scenario, and wrestled alone with the question of who the [Revolutionary] was.

「Kim Dokja thought. That was why the bastard had regressed hundreds of times.」

[Who are y ou.]

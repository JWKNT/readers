The dokkaebi’s voice, ringing out thunderously.

A crowd of low-grade dokkaebis looked down at us from the air, as though watching a house burn. I could see Bihyung among them. When our eyes met, he whistled and pretended to be looking elsewhere.

[I understand just how fucking awful that feels. The despair of thinking, ‘Ah, fuck it. The defeat you must have felt when you realized that the constellation you barely managed to win over by shaking your ass so hard was inferior to the one attached to some bastard who’d been outstanding from the start. Your anger at this unfair world. I know it all too well myself.]

[Some constellations object to the dokkaebi’s speech.]

From the way he spoke, this was no ordinary dokkaebi.

A timid low-grade dokkaebi couldn’t do something like that. They knew their channel would be ruined if they drew even a little backlash from the constellations.

But that applied to small channels. Dokkaebis with large channels had a whole different level of nerve.

They understood the laws of stories.

You couldn’t set a great story in motion by flattering subscribing constellations and catering only to the tastes of a few.

A true storyteller had to engage with the ‘characters,’ rather than the ‘audience.’

I closely observed the dokkaebi as he continued his speech.

[For all of you, I’ve prepared something. An opportunity for those of you who, because you’re unlucky, unfortunate, and haven’t made an effort, are still ‘people who won’t make it’ to become ‘people who will make it’ overnight!]

Wait, could that bastard be······ ‘Dokgak (獨脚)’?

[Now, the boy you’re looking at is nothing less than a walking SSS-grade item! You could say there’s nothing to throw away, from his head to his toes. His name is ‘Myung Ilsang.’ A lucky bastard chosen by the Star Stream who’s been to another world and back. You’ve all imagined it at least once, haven’t you? Being suddenly summoned to another dimension, gaining immense power, spending passionate nights with a cute elf girlfriend, and being hailed as a hero after saving the world! That’s right. This damned bastard is the very ‘disaster’ you’ll be hunting today.]

I’d thought something was strange.

I’d wondered how low-grade dokkaebis could fill the intermediate dokkaebi’s vacant position, but with a dokkaebi from a major channel like that, it was possible.

[I can already hear your complaints. How are you supposed to catch someone wrapped from head to toe in SSS-grade equipment? Haha, please don’t worry. He’s currently under a penalty. His power is sealed. He’s strong, but he’s a treasure vault you can bring down if you all beat him up together.]

“······That fucker gives me the creeps.”

Han Sooyoung clicked her tongue.

As a writer, she seemed to have already grasped the dokkaebi’s intent.

If you called a ‘disaster’ a ‘disaster,’ nobody would come.

But what if you passed that disaster off as a ‘treasure vault’?

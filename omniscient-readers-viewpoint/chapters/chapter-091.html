A Single Story

「It had taken a very long time to reach that decision.」

I looked at Yoo Joonghyuk as he made his quiet declaration.

This hadn’t been the plan. Yoo Joonghyuk was supposed to use the memories of the 999th round to buy time.

He had to provoke the ‘Outer Gods’ just enough to persuade them.

“There is no next round for me.”

Strangely, watching him throw my entire plan into the trash before my eyes didn’t make me angry.

「“Yoo Joonghyuk, who was a regressor.”」

Only now did I truly feel the weight of what he had said then.

Until now, I had believed the Yoo Joonghyuk of this round had grown so quickly because of the information I gave him and other intervening variables. But I couldn’t say that anymore.

The Stories rising from his entire body were proof. Stories bearing the will to live, not the resolve to die.

「Yoo Joonghyuk had staked everything on this round.」

Like a train racing forward on every last bit of its fuel, Yoo Joonghyuk was living with all his strength.

This round was not material for his next life.

【…Captain, you’re lying, right? Huh? You’re joking, aren’t you?】

Lee Jihye’s expression twisted. It wasn’t simply the face of someone whose beliefs had been betrayed. It was the face of someone whose world was collapsing.

Reaching for the last straw of hope she had been seeking.

But Yoo Joonghyuk did not take that hand.

“Lee Jihye. Have I ever lied to you?”

【…Why.】

An explosive current of energy flowed from Lee Jihye’s whole body. I could feel the histories of the 999th round running wild.

【Why Why Why Why Why Why Why Why Why Why Why Why Why Why Why Why Why Why Why Why Why Why Why Why Why Why Why Why Why Why Why Why Why Why Why Why Why Why Why Why Why Why Why Why Why Why Why Why】

The Stories were asking. Why here, instead of the 999th round? Why this world, instead of the one they had lived in?

When I stepped forward, Yoo Joonghyuk stopped me.

“Stay back.”

His cheek, trembling with pain. However hard he tried to hide his feelings, he couldn’t erase his distinctive habits. After a soft sigh, I spoke.

—There’s nowhere left to retreat anyway.

—I’m sorry.

—Forget it. You made your choice.

Yoo Joonghyuk might seem a realist at first glance, but he was more of an idealist than anyone. A man who didn’t pursue ideals would never have kept regressing in the first place. So to protect his ideals, someone had to be a realist.

Watching the furious waves of ‘Outer Gods’ surge toward us, I took another step forward.

[Yoo Joonghyuk did not reject your worldline.]

At the True Voice I released with all my strength, the panicking ‘Outer God kings’ turned toward me. The Lee Jihye, Lee Hyunsung, Kim Namwoon and Uriel of the 999th round…

[Have you forgotten? He was always the one being chosen, not the one choosing.]

Though he had regressed over and over, Yoo Joonghyuk had never once truly wanted to regress.

Even as he abandoned the third round for the fourth, the fourth for the fifth, it hadn’t truly been his will.

Regression was his fate, not his choice. It wasn’t Yoo Joonghyuk who chose, but the ‘story’ that wanted him to regress.

[We don’t need to fight each other. Why must tragedies compete over their suffering?]

I didn’t know whether these words would work. Even so, I had to say them.

[We will not exclude you. We don’t want to remember you as calamities. We—]

I looked up at the sky. The shower of immeasurable gazes, unlike anything I had ever witnessed, made me dizzy. But I didn’t look away.

Just as Yoo Joonghyuk was no longer the Yoo Joonghyuk of the zeroth round, I was no longer the Kim Dokja of Scenario 1.

[We want to stand against that sky together with you.]

Beyond the sky, the stars blazed madly.

[The vast majority of constellations do not understand your words!]

[The Bureau’s Great Dokkaebis are astonished by your thoughts!]

Indirect messages poured down like starlight.

Before, I would have been too busy reading them. But now I knew.

The starlight in that sky was only decoration to hide the darkness there.

[The ‘King of Stories’ is looking at you.]

That was when I heard a very faint laugh.

【Hey, do you even know what that means? Are you saying we should destroy the <Star Stream>?】

A sneering voice. The Kim Namwoon of the 999th round.

【You think we haven’t tried that?】

Deep furrows of emotion lay in his voice.

His face was smiling, but it wasn’t a real smile.

Resignation seeping into that exaggerated laughter.

【We tried too. Brought down every constellation, even killed our worldline’s Dokkaebi King. Know what happened?】

Kim Namwoon walked across the sea until he was right before me.

As though offering a sweet despair, he whispered.

【The world just disappeared.】

I listened silently.

【We cleared the scenario properly and fulfilled every condition…and still lost everything we loved. Without any miracle or reward.】

The words of the Uriel from the 999th round came to mind.

「If the <Star Stream> disappears, the universe turns to chaos. Such a worldline must not be created. That is Evil (惡).」

That was what she had said when I spoke of destroying the <Star Stream>.

Kim Namwoon continued.

【Do you know what we saw at that world’s end?】

[You must have seen an enormous wall. A vast wall enclosing all these worlds, whose beginning and end you couldn’t fathom.]

【…How do you know that?】

[Because our goal is there too. We don’t simply want to destroy the <Star Stream>.]

I repeated what ‘Secretive Plotter’ had told me, in my own voice.

[We want to eliminate the one responsible for all these tragedies, who must be beyond the ‘Final Wall.’]

The Kim Namwoon of the 999th round looked at me as though in deep shock.

His lips moved several times before he shouted.

【No one can cross the ‘Final Wall’! That’s—】

【Namwoon, they possess the ‘last key’ that may let them go beyond the ‘Final Wall.’】

It was Lee Hyunsung.

Confused, the Kim Namwoon of the 999th round looked back and forth between Lee Hyunsung and me.

I nodded toward the Lee Hyunsung of the 999th round.

[The ‘Outer God kings’ are shaken by your words!]

[Your declaration is causing a drastic change in ‘Scenario 98’!]

[Many constellations are outraged by your choice!]

It was all right if we couldn’t bring the stars to our side. All right if no nebula sided with us.

But if these beings took our side.

If the ‘kings’ who had lived the 999th round stayed in this worldline and fought with us—

【…Let’s say you have a ‘key’ like that.】

The instant I heard that voice, an icy chill ran down my spine.

【Then is there any reason I shouldn’t take that ‘key’ from you?】

The instant Kim Namwoon’s dagger darted in at my throat.

Claaang!

A black arc passing before my eyes intercepted it.

Under the heavy impact, Yoo Joonghyuk and Kim Namwoon both fell back a couple of steps.

【Hahaha! Yes! This is the feeling!】

After clashing with the [Black Heavenly Demon Sword], ‘Monarch of the Great Abyss’ was bleeding from the back of his hand.

【Do you know how long I’ve waited? Do you know how many years I’ve wandered to fight you one more time!】

‘Monarch of the Great Abyss.’

A being who had traveled through those distant ages to revive the one he admired once more.

[The Giant Story ‘Delusional Design’ is beginning its story!]

Uriel, supporting the staggering Lee Jihye, had already taken a fighting stance too.

【He told me once. If he ever became something other than himself, he wanted me to kill him with my own hands.】

With a dazzling roar, the sun in the sky shone again.

【It seems that time has come.】

The steel filling my vision blocked the blazing heat pouring down.

—Get away. I cannot stop them alone.

Unlike the others, the Lee Hyunsung of the 999th round had not been summoned as a ‘calamity.’

Which meant he did not have the ‘scenario’s protection’ they did.

“Kim Dokja!”

Almost at the same moment, my companions arrived.

Han Sooyoung, who had flown in on the Black Flame Dragon, asked first.

“What happened?”

“Just what it looks like.”

Han Sooyoung bit down hard on her lip.

I said nothing. Instead, I looked at my companions.

At Jung Heewon, Shin Yoosung, Lee Gilyoung, Lee Jihye and Yoo Sangah.

“This is the last hurdle.”

My companions probably felt it too.

“Please. Don’t die, any of you.”

In this fight, even I couldn’t protect them.

Kwoooooom!

With an impact that seemed to overturn heaven and earth, fierce tidal waves and sunlight crossed. Through the white foam filling my vision, I saw clashing blades.

An ink-black flash cleaving the sea.

Yoo Joonghyuk was fighting. Withstanding the combined attacks of the Kim Namwoon and Uriel from the 999th round, he displayed astonishing martial prowess.

[The Giant Story ‘Lonely Pilgrim of Destruction’ is beginning its story!]

[The Story ‘Hell of Eternity’ is beginning its story!]

Power enough to face two ‘kings.’

That was Yoo Joonghyuk’s true strength.

All the Stories he had built were exploding at once.

[The connection of ‘Disconnected Film Theory’ is incomplete!]

[Cracks are forming in the continuity of the Stories!]

But that balance couldn’t last forever.

“Dokja-ssi.”

I turned to find Jung Heewon there. Dazzling wings of an archangel were blossoming from her back.

Beast Lord Shin Yoosung, Maritime Admiral Lee Jihye, Steel Sword Emperor Lee Hyunsung.

Companions who had lived lives different from those in the original novel, and were therefore moving toward different answers.

We glanced at one another and nodded.

“I’ll help Joonghyuk-ssi. The rest of you, take that side!”

[The constellation ‘Demon-like Judge of Fire’ releases her Status!]

Spreading her wings, Jung Heewon flew toward Yoo Joonghyuk under Uriel’s protection.

It seemed to be Uriel’s choice. Now that she knew the ‘Outer God king’ was her own self in the distant future…

【I told you. You cannot face me without a nebula’s protection.】

[Flames of Retribution] rose as though to pierce the sky, then fell toward the sea.

But I had no time to worry about Jung Heewon. Someone was pouring immense Status toward us.

Through the bursting waves, the Lee Jihye of the 999th round stared at us with empty eyes.

“Everyone, dodge!”

Stepping forward, Yoo Sangah twisted spacetime. But with that ‘Outer God king’ fighting in earnest, Sakyamuni’s power was useless.

With a cracking sound, the twisted space was forcibly unfolded.

The battleship of the 999th round’s Lee Jihye was rising from the sea. ‘Master of the Sunken Island.’ Her [Turtle Dragon] looked less like a battleship than a literal island.

“Ajusshi!”

Sensing danger, Shin Yoosung had her [Chimera Dragon] unleash its breath. Then all the sea beasts hiding underwater surged out.

Gyaaaaaah!

Roaring fiercely, the monsters charged at the battleship’s outer hull. But the island was too enormous for them to stop. With a crunching sound, the monsters were crushed. The surrounding sea heaved heavily, and tidal waves swept in.

“Hyung, get back! Hurry!”

“Kim Dokja, get the hell back!”

I knew very well what they feared too.

Beyond the tidal wave, the Lee Jihye of the 999th round was shouting.

The only one of my ‘Giant Stories’ with the power to face an ‘Outer God king.’

Limited Random Item Box.

According to the lore of ‘Ways of Survival,’ this was a coin item that had been sold as a limited edition in a past ‘scenario.’

[W-wait, wh-why is this here?]

Biryu, who appeared a moment later, also let out a cry of astonishment.

[Th-th-that should have been banned from sale almost immediately!]

According to the original ‘Ways of Survival,’ this item had a fairly complicated backstory.

Released long before the scenario for planetary system 8612 even began, this coin item had been forcibly recalled by the Star Stream’s Bureau.

With good reason: the fact that inserting a ‘lower-tier item’ would always produce a ‘higher-tier item’ had consequences so far-reaching that it threatened the balance of the scenarios.

What was more, a single item box cost a staggering 1 million coins.

The constellations were outraged by this absurd pricing policy, and the stupid dokkaebi who had devised the item was fired from the Bureau.

[C-constellations-nim. That, you seeee······. I don’t really know why that’s there eith······ E-ehehehe! Broadcast over!]

[Channel #BI-7623 is temporarily closing.]

When Biryu vanished after babbling nonsense to himself, the voices of the constellations disappeared as well.

It was a shame I couldn’t see the constellations’ reactions anymore, but, well, what could I do?

Rrrrr.

I looked down at the box, which was vibrating on its own. The random draw was about to begin in earnest.

[A sword-type item was inserted. An item of the same type will appear as the reward!]

[The random draw is beginning!]

The Limited Random Item Box randomly spat out a higher-grade item related to whatever had been put inside.

That meant that, in terms of probability, anything from C-grade to SSS-grade could come out.

In terms of probability, that is.

[The item offered as a sacrifice is associated with a particular constellation!]

[The probability of an item associated with that constellation appearing has increased greatly.]

···Huh?

That was an unexpected message. Still, it didn’t seem likely to work against me. Sweat pooled in my clenched hands. Even buying random-draw items in online games had never made me this nervous. Please, just give me something around A-grade.

[A higher-grade item has appeared!]

[The Random Item Box has 0 uses remaining.]

I glanced at Yoo Sangah and Lee Gilyoung in turn. Their eyes were shining.

“Shall we open it?”

“Yes!”

We opened the box.

“W-woooow!”

Lee Gilyoung’s scream was so loud that it startled even me. But what was inside really was that astonishing.

A pure white blade (劍身) extending from an elegant, ink-black guard······ But didn’t its shape look a little like ‘Broken Faith’?

As an additional effect, equipping it raises Constitution and Strength by 2 levels each.

I hadn’t gotten an item with a mere letter grade, but an actual Star Relic (星遺物)?

In the world of ‘Ways of Survival,’ ‘Star Relics’ were the only items excluded from the grading chart. This wasn’t simply because they had powerful abilities, but because these items were very special.

Every Star Relic held the power a constellation had possessed while alive.

Their capabilities varied enormously depending on the world in which the constellation had been a hero and how widely known that constellation was, but simply containing a constellation’s power gave a Star Relic tremendous value.

And it even raised Constitution and Strength by 2 each.

Considering that even an overall stat boost of just 1 was enough to elevate an item to A-grade, its additional effects alone made it equivalent to at least an S-grade item.

Even Yoo Joonghyuk probably hadn’t obtained an item this good yet.

“······It’s all right if I take it, isn’t it?”

“Of course. Naturally, it’s yours, Dokja-ssi.”

Yoo Sangah, insisting as if to settle the matter firmly in advance. Lee Gilyoung was nodding enthusiastically, too.

I glanced at Han Myungoh, but he was merely gnawing on a ground rat’s leg with a vacant expression, muttering something unintelligible as he did. I’d been certain he would spout more nonsense about taking it for himself······ How strange.

[You have acquired a Star Relic.]

[The owner of the Star Relic is curious about you.]

Great Apocalypse

[Your ■■ is approaching.]

A system message ringing in my ears.

I had been hearing it over and over for the past few days.

“It seems there isn’t much time left.”

“No.”

My mother and I sat across the table from each other, drinking tea. For a moment, we watched the panel installed in the [Industrial Complex]’s reception room.

—The American continent destroyed! Where will the ‘Outer Gods’ strike next?

—Emergency evacuation orders issued across Northeast Asia!

—Nebulae abandon Earth. “Nowhere to run, no matter where we flee.”

The news showed the Korean Peninsula last.

The entire peninsula was crowded with refugees from around the world.

I knew very well what they expected to find here.

[The next Great Destruction Scenario region is ‘Northeast Asia.’]

[6 days, 8 hours and 24 minutes remain until the Great Destruction Scenario begins.]

On the screen, my mother spoke in my place as the [Industrial Complex]’s representative.

The [Industrial Complex] does not turn away new citizens. However…

My mother said with a wry smile.

“It’s embarrassing to watch.”

“It suits you. You look like a president.”

In fact, it would be fair to say my mother, not I, was now the [Industrial Complex]’s master. Its citizens probably followed her more readily than me too.

“Show your face to the people of Seoul before you leave. Even a simple greeting from you will give them a great deal of strength.”

Indeed, I could hear reporters shouting through megaphones outside the [Industrial Complex].

—Demon King of Salvation-nim! Is it true you’ve returned?

—Demon King of Salvation-nim! Please say a few words about your plan to stop the apocalypse!

…A plan.

I smiled wryly, just like my mother.

“If that’s a mascot’s duty.”

Then we sipped our tea.

A dark, murky sky. A lightning strike splitting the peninsula in two at any moment wouldn’t have seemed out of place.

“It’s peaceful.”

“It is.”

Even so, that was what we said.

Tea leaves swayed in the cups. Such a leisurely teatime. It was the first in our thirty-year history as mother and son.

The time we had wanted so much had only arrived now, on the eve of everything’s destruction.

My mother asked me nothing.

What I would do next, what I sought at the end of this story—she asked about none of it. I knew that was her way.

“I’ll be going, then.”

“The ‘Heavenly Wind God’ is looking for you. Be sure to stop by once more before you leave.”

…Pungbaek? Why was he looking for me again?

I remembered the unpleasant incident during the Great War of Saints and Demons. Did he want another round before the final scenario? For now, I nodded lightly and stepped outside.

Someone was waiting for me there.

[You’ve done well to make it this far, Kim Dokja.]

It was Bihyung, looking prosperous since becoming a Great Dokkaebi. The long coat made of white tiger fur suited him rather well.

I asked sarcastically,

“What made you wait for once?”

[The reunion between you and your mother is a popular Story among the subscribing constellations. I couldn’t interrupt the flow.]

Bihyung shrugged and jabbered on.

[The constellation ‘Demon-like Judge of Fire’ dabs at her eyes.]

[The constellation ‘Abyssal Black Flame Dragon’ grumbles as he hands over a handkerchief.]

Apparently, he had been broadcasting that too. Damn bastard.

[The final scenario is right around the corner now.]

“I know.”

[I’m sure you do. As you know, the final scenario…]

“Bihyung.”

At my words, he stopped and looked at me.

“Why did you choose us?”

A faint ripple passed through Bihyung’s eyes.

I already knew which scenario window would be floating before him now.

Final Dragon

Metatron stared at the ruined conference chamber. The Demon Kings and archangels who had held weapons only moments ago now lay strewn about.

Stories of Good and Evil scattering.

The effects of Chaos exceeding its critical point were reaching inside the gray sphere.

One of the angels still conscious reached toward him.

[Scribe……]

With a crunch, Michael’s rear foot crushed the angel’s head. Kicking aside the dead angel, Michael pulled out the child-sized archangel he had hidden against his chest.

The unconscious archangel dangled from Michael’s grasp.

[Should I kill Raphael, too? It feels a little wasteful to let him go like this……]

[You may spare him if you wish. Chaos has reached its limit.]

[Then what about that Demon King?]

Metatron looked toward the edge of the sphere, where a fierce exchange still raged. Agares stood there, his entire body in tatters.

Asmodeus and the other ‘Seekers of the End’ were attacking together, but the Demon King Agares had yet to fall. With Stories streaming from his entire body and a vengeful spirit’s hatred etched into his eyes.

Even amid that fierce battle, Agares still held cigarettes between his lips. Not just one, but several.

[The stigma ‘One Ciggie for Herculean Strength Lv.???’ is active.]

[The stigma ‘One Ciggie for Agility Lv.???’ is active.]

[The stigma ‘One Ciggie for Magic Power Lv.???’ is active.]

The specialty of Agares, a longtime chain-smoker. A stigma unique to Agares, harboring a Story that overclocked his incarnation body’s performance.

[Indeed, the fame of the ‘Ruler of Eastern Hell’ is well earned. But how long can you hold on?]

Without replying, Agares took out a fresh cigarette and lit it.

Metatron and Michael were approaching him across the now-cleared battlefield.

Agares said,

[Metatron. Think again. This cannot preserve Good and Evil. What meaning is there in being remembered after everyone is annihilated?]

[That is who we are, Ruler of Eastern Hell.]

Ancient Stories flowed behind Metatron.

They were Stories he had read, he had lived, and he had believed in.

[The oldest Good smiles.]

That was the ‘Scribe of Heaven.’

The one who recorded this world’s Good.

The being who decided what was Good, and served as its standard.

Looking at his longtime nemesis, Agares, too, felt the Story flowing leisurely beside him.

[The Most Ancient Evil tilts its head.]

It was the long history of Evil he had followed.

A history of opposing Good, being excluded, and being punished.

At that moment, Agares felt thousands of years of his life condensed into a single comma.

This flow would never end.

As long as the ‘Ruler of Eastern Hell’ lived and the ‘Scribe of Heaven’ existed. As long as they opposed one another and repeated their wars.

Even if Metatron and Agares died, others would become the ‘Scribe of Heaven’ and the ‘Ruler of Eastern Hell.’

[If that’s what passes for Good and Evil……]

[Then I quit being Evil.]

A cigarette flew from his fingertips.

The cigarette spinning through the air, trailing smoke.

Asmodeus shouted urgently,

[Stop him!]

Swirling smoke enveloped Agares’s body.

[The stigma ‘One Ciggie for Cowardice Lv.???’ activates!]

Metatron looked down at the cigarette on the floor. Acrid smoke from the still-burning cigarette lingered in the air.

His rival was gone. Good was now alone.

Was this loneliness, or a kind of liberation? Metatron could not tell.

[The Most Ancient Evil takes an interest in a new Evil.]

[How unfortunate. I had intended to take Agares’s ‘Wall.’]

[You will have it soon.]

[Current Chaos: 100]

Destruction would soon begin.

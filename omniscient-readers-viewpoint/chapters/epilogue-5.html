She imagined the countless Kim Dokjas scattered across worldlines. People who must have been born in different circumstances, in different forms.

Meeting them or bringing them back meant nothing now.

All the companions could hope for now was a miracle.

A miracle that would bring back the Kim Dokja they remembered.

Even if it was fiction. Even if it was a lie. If only he would imagine his own happiness.

If all those 'Kim Dokjas' would imagine a single universe.

“Sooyoung-ssi.”

“I know. That's why I'm asking you.”

“······What do you need us to do?”

Instead of answering, Han Sooyoung set a laptop on the small desk in the hospital room and opened a text file. The companions knew the file well.

A novel still titled 'Untitled'.

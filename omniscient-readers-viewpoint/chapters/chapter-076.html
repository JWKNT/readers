Book of Revelation

—Ruuumble!

On the screen, the constellations of Good and Evil were unleashing their ferocity at one another. The tension stretched taut as a rubber band about to snap. Maintaining that balance was a single nebula, the smallest of them all, yet shining brightly.

[It has been a very long time since I last saw Good and Evil gathered together……]

Sakyamuni, Mandala’s Guardian, watched the screen with a peculiar expression. Ancient memories drifted through his eyes.

The scenario before the scenarios. The days when Good, Evil, and Neutrality had all stood together.

The story of how they had faced the dragon of destruction together to prevent the world’s end……

—I want to help my companions, too.

At those words, Sakyamuni turned toward the small tank beside him. Inside it, the small form of a soul glowed white, humming as it spoke.

—When will I be able to reincarnate?

[That is not your battlefield, child. You will be reborn as a being who serves a greater purpose.]

—They are my purpose.

Even after becoming a soul, Yoo Sangah spoke with a resolute voice.

—If I can’t save them here, my reincarnation won’t mean anything.

[Purpose……]

Sakyamuni turned to look at another tank across from hers.

Inside it lay the incarnation body of a woman in Buddhist robes.

[You will inhabit the body of a child I cherished.]

—Enter someone else’s body? I thought I was reincarnating.

[You will reincarnate with that body as your incarnation body.]

—Then what about its original owner?

Sakyamuni did not answer.

Could even a Buddha feel sorrow?

Something suddenly dawned on Yoo Sangah.

—Is that person your ‘purpose’?

Sakyamuni silently gazed at the woman in robes inside the tank.

[She has merely returned to the order of the universe. Everything is but the empty turning of a wheel.]

—Is that really what you want to say? She was someone you cherished.

[You will soon understand, child. Such is the life of a reincarnator.]

—I’m not a reincarnator yet.

[You will learn that such bonds mean nothing. How futile everything you held dear truly was.]

—……Is cursing other people a hobby of yours?

[I am telling you the truth, child.]

Sakyamuni looked at the battlefield on the screen.

There stood constellations who had lived for a very long time.

[Constellations suffer from sleeplessness all their lives. They cannot sleep without scenarios, and even in their dreams, they devour the Stories of others. Through that consumption, they wish to erase the scenarios in which they find themselves. And they are always anxious, without even knowing why.]

Sakyamuni, a constellation older than any of them, continued.

[They cannot escape the scenarios, as though trapped in an eternal daydream. They turn away from death and therefore do not know it. And because they do not know death, they cannot awaken from the delusion of the scenarios. They mistakenly believe there exists a single story that will save them.]

On the screen, countless indirect messages appeared from constellations supporting or opposing <Kim Dokja’s Company>.

Sakyamuni slowly shifted his gaze toward the edge of the screen.

[But reincarnators are different.]

The island’s reincarnators were there.

Those who had followed <Kim Dokja’s Company>. And those who still sided with Good or Evil and were being used by the Giant Stories……

Sakyamuni looked at them and said,

[Like constellations, reincarnators live for eternity, but they die and are born again. Knowing death, they know awakening. Knowing awakening, they realize they are merely insignificant components of the scenarios. To reincarnate is to understand the essence of the scenarios.]

Reincarnators of low Status lost their memories upon death, but not all of them did. Some, like Nirvana, reincarnated with memories of their past lives. They continued the scenarios, reborn as various species and genders.

As humans. As frogs. As orcs. As elves. As ants……

Perhaps those expressions were what they had gained at the end of countless reincarnations.

—They all look resigned.

[Because they know nothing will change, whoever wins.]

—We can change the scenarios. We always have.

[But that does not change the fact that they are ‘scenarios.’]

—So you give up? Because a scenario is a scenario, whatever you do? That’s running away. Admitting defeat without even fighting.

[Child, those words insult the lives of reincarnators. Through countless lives, they have struggled against the scenarios—]

—Have you fought with everything you had, without giving up on a single life?

At those words, Sakyamuni fell silent.

Had he never given up on a single life?

Before he could answer, Yoo Sangah continued.

—There’s someone who fought through over eighteen hundred lives without giving up.

Yoo Sangah looked at the screen.

A man in a black coat stood there.

—And someone who watched all those lives with him.

Beside him, a man in a white coat was looking at his companions.

The last person his gaze settled on was the fallen Lee Hyunsung.

[I have lived far too long to keep count. Yet there is one thing I can still count.]

Looking at Lee Hyunsung, Sakyamuni said,

[One more reincarnator to join this island.]

“Not yet.”

I checked the fallen Lee Hyunsung’s pulse. There was none. No breath passed through his nose, and when I lifted his eyelids, only the whites of his eyes showed.

“……Really?”

Jung Heewon looked at me as though she wanted to believe in a miracle.

Seeing her hair bleached white, I could guess what had happened here.

“He definitely isn’t dead.”

My companions’ faces showed mixed feelings. Lee Jihye looked as though she thought I was lying out of kindness, while Lee Gilyoung seemed willing to believe me even if I was.

Han Sooyoung asked,

“Have you decided to change the definition of death now, too?”

“If Hyunsung-ssi were dead, the Master of Steel would have left the scenario, too.”

I looked up into the air. There were no indirect messages, but the Master of Steel had not yet left the scenario.

Jung Heewon grabbed my arm urgently.

“Then what on earth is happening to Hyunsung-ssi—”

“Just as you awakened, Heewon-ssi, Hyunsung-ssi has awakened, too.”

I watched the faint Story fragments flowing across Lee Hyunsung’s skin.

Stories of steel.

It was hard to tell from the outside, but Lee Hyunsung’s body would be filled to the brim with Stories of steel right now.

[Character ‘Lee Hyunsung’ is on the verge of an attribute evolution.]

There was a reason Lee Hyunsung was called ‘The Greatest Shield’ in the original.

When he gave his life to protect someone, the ‘Steel Sword Emperor’ would reach the final stage of Steel Transformation.

When he regained consciousness, Lee Hyunsung would be the toughest shield in the world.

Jung Heewon asked in a trembling voice,

“Then, then he’s alive?”

“Yes.”

“Really? You’re not lying?”

Tears ran down Jung Heewon’s cheeks as she broke down. She laid a hand on Lee Hyunsung’s chest. A heart that did not beat. Feeling that indifferent silence, she struggled to continue.

“But I can’t hear anything……”

“You won’t hear anything in the future, either.”

“……What?”

I looked down at Lee Hyunsung.

His heart had hardened like one hundred percent pure steel. It would never beat again.

Jung Heewon wouldn’t understand what that meant yet……

“But Hyunsung-ssi is definitely alive. Don’t worry.”

“At any rate, he won’t be of any use right now.”

Yoo Joonghyuk, his Status released, continued dispassionately.

“Everyone, get a grip. This isn’t the time to grieve.”

Ruuumble!

Across the battlefield, two forces were glaring at our nebula.

Good on one side, Evil on the other. To us, they were simply enemies.

At the center of the two forces stood Metatron, the ‘Scribe of Heaven,’ and Agares, the ‘Ruler of Eastern Hell.’

[Who defeated Barbatos?]

A murmur spread across the battlefield at the question.

Barbatos, the eighth-ranked Demon King, was dead.

Yet the Demon Kings looked amused rather than surprised.

[Interfering with the ‘Great War of Saints and Demons’? You must be out of your minds.]

They looked at us with mockery in their eyes. Sneers full of certainty that although the likes of us had survived by luck so far, we would not be so lucky again.

As they suspected, the members of <Kim Dokja’s Company> were in no condition to fight properly. Yoo Joonghyuk had nearly exhausted his magic power fighting Indra, and Han Sooyoung was worn out from battling the archangels.

Not to mention the unconscious Lee Hyunsung or the exhausted Jung Heewon.

The children who had been to ‘Next City’ were the only ones who might be of help.

“Don’t worry, ajusshi. I’ll wipe them all out.”

Lee Jihye, thumping her chest as she spoke, and Shin Yoosung, nodding beside her, looked incredibly dependable. As I had expected, the children seemed to have returned from ‘Next City’ after undergoing terrifying growth. Lee Gilyoung’s eyes were shining, too.

“Hyung, who should I kill first? Who gives the most EXP?”

Despite our overwhelming disadvantage, he sounded as though he was enjoying a game.

[The constellation sponsor of incarnation ‘Lee Gilyoung’ looks at you.]

I shook my head.

No. It wasn’t time to use Lee Gilyoung yet. And even if I did, I couldn’t be certain of victory.

Anna Croft, who stood beside me, asked,

“Are you really going to fight? You know you have no chance, don’t you?”

Her intentions in asking were obvious.

She already belonged to the ‘Good’ camp. If things went wrong, her best option would be to stab me in the back and join them.

“We’ve never had a chance. I do intend to fight. And I’m confident we’ll win. Provided you don’t betray us, of course.”

At the word betrayal, Anna Croft glared at me and raised one hand.

Selena Kim and Iris, who had been standing in formation behind her, stepped forward.

[The nebula <Asgard> supports <Kim Dokja’s Company>.]

Shocked constellations and Demon Kings shouted.

[Asgard, have you lost your minds?]

[It seems the god of the hammer has finally smashed his own head.]

[God of pranks! Surely you don’t intend to cause trouble here as well?]

Amid the clamor of True Voices, some merely grinned as though events were taking an interesting turn.

One was Marbas, the fifth-ranked Demon King, the ‘Black Mane Lion.’

[A foolish choice, <Asgard>. Your nebula is powerful, but few of your constellations are participating. Far too few to turn the tide of this battle!]

“It’s not just one nebula.”

[Then who else is there? <Kim Dokja’s Company>? You wish to call that little club with only one constellation—you—a ‘nebula’?]

Loud laughter rose among the Demon Kings. Then—

[The constellation ‘Father of the Rich Night’ regards the constellations with a cold gaze.]

[The nebula <Underworld> supports <Kim Dokja’s Company>.]

The laughter abruptly ceased.

[……<Underworld>?]

[<Olympus>! What is the meaning of this? Aren’t they a subordinate nebula of yours?]

At those words, one side of the battlefield opened, revealing <Olympus>.

As expected, it seemed they had joined this scenario, too.

The constellation at their head was familiar to us.

[Hmm, this is awkward…… We can’t reenact <Gigantomachia> here, either.]

Dionysus, the ‘God of Wine and Ecstasy,’ smiled at me as though he was genuinely troubled.

“Dionysus. Will you fight us?”

[Whew. You really make a man crave a drink.]

Dionysus took a bottle from inside his clothes and gulped down the wine.

[Ah, to hell with it. I’ll think about it after I get a little drunk. Demon King of Salvation, come have a drink, too. We have plenty to talk about, don’t we?]

“Thank you for the offer, but I’m afraid now isn’t a good time.”

With a faint chuckle, Dionysus raised his bottle to me. That was answer enough from <Olympus>.

They would not support us, but neither would they show us hostility.

As a vast nebula suddenly suspended its participation, bewilderment spread through the camps of Good and Evil.

I seized the opening.

“Looks like we’re done introducing the players. Shall we get started?”

The constellations and Demon Kings of both forces erupted in fury at my provocation.

Perhaps she hadn’t expected me to be so direct. Beside me, Anna Croft looked as though she was questioning my sanity.

Han Sooyoung said,

“I expected a prophet to be quicker on the uptake. Just watch.”

Anna Croft closed her mouth at Han Sooyoung’s rebuke.

Then one Demon King drew a pitch-black blade and stepped forward.

Even as the situation threatened to explode, Agares and Metatron merely remained silent.

[Many Demon Kings show intense hostility toward you!]

The instant the Demon King’s sword swept toward me with a sharp whoosh, a message rang out.

[Members of the same camp have clashed!]

[The increase in Chaos is accelerating!]

[Chaos has increased by 1.]

[Current Chaos: 76]

The startled Demon King blinked.

In the distance, I could see Metatron and Agares’s faces stiffen. They were looking at the night sky of the <Star Stream>. They had probably realized it.

I spoke in my True Voice to draw the constellations’ attention.

[The ones you’re about to fight are neither Good nor Evil.]

<Kim Dokja’s Company> contained both Good and Evil.

Showing hostility toward our nebula violated the very nature of the ‘Great War of Saints and Demons,’ a war between Good and Evil.

[You could kill us if you tried. But what would happen to you?]

Clouds bearing the energy of Chaos swirled in the distant sky.

Once Chaos exceeded 80, the countdown to destruction would begin.

From this point onward, it was a game of chicken.

[The oldest Good glares at you.]

[The Most Ancient Evil glares at you.]

A game in which the first side to lose its nerve and back down would lose for certain.

[Which would happen first? All of us dying, or the Apocalypse Dragon destroying you? Aren’t you curious?]

I smiled as I drew [Unbreakable Faith].

[I’m very curious.]

Translated, my declaration meant this:

Kill us, and you will certainly die, too.

The constellations were shaken at first, then murmured, then fell silent. Some looked at Agares, others at Metatron. The two main decision-makers on this battlefield. Yet those decision-makers merely kept their silence, their eyes unreadable.

When no orders came, the threat of war unexpectedly began to stir slowly from the fringes.

[<Kim Dokja’s Company>. We understand what you’re thinking.]

The True Voice came from a constellation of <Papyrus>.

[But we have a debt to repay you.]

“Well, I’m not sure who owes whom.”

At my reply, the constellations of <Papyrus> drew their weapons in unison.

[You said you were neither Good nor Evil. But that means you are both Good and Evil.]

The nebula <Papyrus> had chosen the ‘Evil’ camp.

[At the very least, we’ll kill every one of you who chose ‘Good.’]

It was a wise choice. Chaos only increased when Good fought Good or Evil fought Evil. <Papyrus> had found a way to judge us without violating this battlefield’s rules.

[The Most Ancient Evil wishes to eliminate you.]

[The oldest Good wishes to eliminate you.]

In this ‘Great War of Saints and Demons,’ we were nothing but a virus. Hosts that disrupted a functioning system and spread disease.

The wave of Good and Evil gathering around <Papyrus> was growing larger.

The constellations who had been burning with hostility toward one another just moments ago were all turning that hostility on us.

Ruuumble!

My companions’ expressions stiffened. Changing his stance, Yoo Joonghyuk said,

“Kim Dokja.”

Yoo Joonghyuk would know, too. Even with <Underworld> and <Asgard> on our side, even with the members of <Kim Dokja’s Company> all gathered together—

If we met them head-on, at least one of us would certainly die.

My perception suddenly accelerated, and time slowed ever so slightly.

「They’re coming faster than I expected.」

「Many constellations made up their minds too quickly.」

「Should I have pushed Chaos to 80 first?」

I thought of ‘Ways of Survival.’

Whose help could get us through this?

My adoptive parents in <Underworld>?

The Great Sage, Heaven’s Equal, who had yet to join the battle?

Cheok Jungyeong and the constellations of the Korean Peninsula?

The faces of my teachers and Jang Hayoung came to mind, too.

Especially Jang Hayoung.

Desperate as I was for help, part of me hoped Jang Hayoung would not come.

[The ‘Fruit of Good and Evil’ stimulates your guilt.]

Perhaps this was a sense of responsibility.

The wish that Jang Hayoung, born into this world because of me, would not be swept up in the scenarios.

The hope that she would live her own story, entirely of her own free will.

Those conflicting feelings were why I seldom invited Jang Hayoung to <Kim Dokja’s Company> or provided information about the future.

“Aaaaaagh!”

The ranks of reincarnators meeting the enemy on the front lines were collapsing. Swept up in the tsunami, they screamed as they were torn apart.

[Die!]

Watching the constellations and Demon Kings charge with their clichéd lines, Han Sooyoung smiled tensely.

“We’ve won. The ones who start spouting lines like that always croak first.”

At her joke, my companions forced up the corners of their mouths.

“They’re coming.”

Their lines were clichéd, but their Status was anything but.

Good and Evil was the most clichéd Story in this world, yet also one of the most powerful.

The tension prickling across my skin was unlike anything I had felt on any battlefield before.

This was real.

This was the Great War of Saints and Demons. The true power of the constellations.

Kraaaash!

Three hundred meters.

Two hundred meters.

One hundred meters.

“Now.”

[The narrators of the nebula <Kim Dokja’s Company> have gathered.]

[The Story ‘Torch That Swallowed the Myth’ begins its storytelling!]

「Demon Realm’s Spring」 wrapped around us protectively, while 「Torch That Swallowed the Myth」 roared as though ready to tear apart everything approaching. This alone would not be enough to stop them.

They might all be ‘Giant Stories,’ but the years they had accumulated were different. A tremendous gulf that fifty years in Kaizenix could not bridge.

Nevertheless, this was our story.

“Wait!”

“What are you doing, ajusshi!”

The others were just as startled by my bizarre behavior.

We might die even if we fought for our lives, and now I was getting in the way?

Instead of answering, I pointed ahead.

“Huh?”

Metatron and Agares.

“What do you think?”

Talks between Good and Evil. It sounded fine. But…… the Metatron I knew would never back down at a time like this. He would accept any sacrifice to put Absolute Good into full practice.

“I don’t know what they’re plotting, but there is a way to find out what’ll happen next.”

“What is it?”

“Damn it. Right, we had that option.”

Talks or war, it would all be settled within the next nine hours.

And we knew someone who could read that sort of near future better than anyone else in this world.

He really was quick-witted about things like this. He had found a solution before we did. Han Sooyoung ground her teeth in frustration. Yet Yoo Joonghyuk did not look triumphant at all.

“To get straight to the point, I couldn’t read the future.”

Come to think of it, Anna Croft rarely managed to foresee futures involving me. I remembered that it was probably because of the [Fourth Wall]. What had she said? That the future was full of noise, as though someone had scribbled on it?

But Anna Croft shook her head.

“It isn’t noise in the future. I can’t read it at all. It’s not that someone scribbled on the page. The page with the scribbles doesn’t exist in the first place.”

Han Sooyoung and I looked at each other.

A sense of foreboding crept over me, very slowly.

“Kim Dokja, this……”

Not scribbles on a page, but the page itself disappearing.

No matter how I thought about it, there was only one kind of future like that.

“……Surely not?”

[Chaos increases by 1.]

[Current Chaos: 83]

“It hasn’t been thirty minutes yet.”

Chaos was rising even though the time had not elapsed.

[Members of the same camp have clashed!]

[Current Chaos: 84]

Someone was trying to destroy this world.

[Members of the same camp have clashed!]

[Current Chaos: 85]

“……Who the hell is it?”

Han Sooyoung forced out the question.

But none of us could answer.

“The kids aren’t causing trouble, are they?”

“They aren’t you.”

Children or not, they wouldn’t act so recklessly in a situation like this.

Though Gilyoung did worry me a little……

“Anna Croft.”

“I’m looking.”

Even if the pages of the future had been torn out, the events before those pages disappeared should still remain. Just as even a misprinted book had to wait some time before being discarded.

[Chaos is increasing!]

“We don’t have time to sit around waiting.”

Yoo Joonghyuk was the first to move.

Sweat beaded on Anna Croft’s forehead as she searched desperately for the pages of the future.

In the end, Han Sooyoung and I decided to act, too.

“Anna, tell us by Sound Transmission when you find out.”

Our companions were already on deck, having sensed that something was wrong.

“Dokja-ssi, what’s happening?”

“Some people are attacking members of their own camp.”

“Huh? Why would they do that?”

Lee Jihye frowned as though she couldn’t make sense of it.

“You said we’d all die if Chaos went any higher. That’s why the angels and Demon Kings went in there……”

“Could they have the same goal as us?”

“If they did, they wouldn’t be raising Chaos now of all times.”

“Then don’t tell me……?”

“We have to stop them, no matter what. Something truly terrible will happen if we don’t.”

“What lunatics…… No, why the hell would they?”

Why try to bring about the world’s destruction?

It was difficult to give a precise answer.

But in the <Star Stream>, there was one universal answer to every incomprehensible situation.

“……There are a great many kinds of ‘Stories’ in the world.”

‘Good and Evil’ were not all that existed here. Just as <Kim Dokja’s Company> was neither Good nor Evil, some pursued Stories we could never relate to.

Some lived to prevent destruction. Others lived for it.

Tssssss……!

The probability in the air fluctuated erratically. Sparks leaped up across the battlefield.

Already standing on the figurehead, Yoo Joonghyuk seemed to have detected the largest of them.

“Five locations in all. Split up.”

I gave the others instructions.

“Sooyoung, go east. Yoosung, Jihye, and Gilyoung, take the south. Heewon-ssi, stay with the ship, just in case.”

“What about you, Dokja-ssi?”

But the ‘Brash Swamp Predator’, already out of his fucking mind, didn't hesitate.

[······Manners? Manners, my ass······!]

As the ‘Brash Swamp Predator’, now right in front of me, released his ‘Status’, the other constellations unleashed theirs at the same time. Just as powerful sparks burst into the air, heralding a collision of ‘Status’.

[The wish right used by the constellation ‘Demon King of Salvation’ takes effect.]

Bzzzzzt!

Dazzling sparks covered the entire hall, and the agitated constellations stopped moving for an instant. As everyone hesitated, a portal opened in the ceiling.

An ominous aura surged beyond the portal.

It wasn't an ‘Outer God’.

But clearly, there was certainly something there every bit as tremendous.

Who was it? At this level, it had to be a constellation of the highest rank, or······.

Rrrrrumble······.

With an explosion that seemed to tear the air apart, an unfamiliar being emerged through the portal.

A being wrapped from head to toe in an utterly mysterious aura.

Yet that being was neither a god nor a constellation.

A man who appeared in black clothes like a magician's, leaning on an antique staff.

[······A Great Dokkaebi?]

It was a Great Dokkaebi.

[It has been a long time, constellations.]

With that single sentence, the hall fell silent as though doused in cold water.

It was an enforced silence. The constellations releasing their Status and those shouting all held their breath.

[The rules of the <Star Stream> bind every constellation in the ‘Gourmet Association’.]

Bzzzzzt!

Enough probability to bind every constellation present.

Even with the authority to operate a channel, an ordinary dokkaebi could never have done this.

[U-uuugh······.]

Even the ‘Brash Swamp Predator’, who hadn't yielded to the threats of high-ranking constellations, was backing away, his face drained white.

I had read a description of that dokkaebi in the original novel.

「Most incarnations believe there are only four dokkaebi ranks. Low, intermediate, quasi-high, and high. But there are dokkaebis above them as well.」

「The leaders (首座) of the dokkaebis, bearing horns in twelve colors, who command the dokkaebis of the <Star Stream>.」

Seven red horns protruded from the dokkaebi's head.

The <Star Stream> called them ‘Great Dokkaebis’.

[My apologies, but the party ends here.]

●

Back when I was engrossed in ‘Ways of Survival’, I had once wondered about the relationship between constellations and dokkaebis.

However special the dokkaebis' powers might be, a high-ranking constellation could kill one or two of them without any trouble. Yet even constellations with that much power didn't casually lay hands on ‘dokkaebis’. No matter how irritating a scenario the dokkaebis opened, the constellations never killed them.

······Why?

The reason was right before my eyes.

Bzzzzzz!

With a single gesture, a net of probability spread out and ensnared the entire ‘Gourmet Association’. Sparks of a density I had never seen before.

This, too, was the power of a ‘Story’.

A ‘Story’ probably borrowed from the most powerful being in this world.

Narrative-grade or Historical-grade, anyone who tried to leave would surely be swept up in that storm of sparks and annihilated.

Even high-ranking constellations such as ‘Thursday's Thunder’ and the ‘Morning Star Goddess’ merely looked deeply displeased, without voicing any complaint.

Persephone was the first to speak.

[Great Dokkaebi ‘Halong’. It has been a long time.]

The Great Dokkaebi slowly turned toward us.

[It has indeed, Queen of the Underworld.]

[Is the ‘King of Stories’ well?]

[His Majesty is in good health.]

Perhaps it was because of who she was dealing with; a tension I had never seen before lingered in Persephone's expression.

[What brings you here? And with the dokkaebis of the Enforcement Division, no less······.]

The dokkaebis of the [Enforcement Division] I had seen before were forming ranks behind the Great Dokkaebi. The terrifying presence they radiated rivaled that of the constellations in the ‘Gourmet Association’. That was only natural.

The dokkaebis of the Enforcement Division had once been ‘constellations’ themselves.

Instead of answering Persephone immediately, the Great Dokkaebi surveyed the constellations with quiet eyes.

[I must escort away the esteemed constellations who have taken out ‘coin’ loans.]

[······Coins?]

At that moment, several constellations groaned and began fleeing.

The ‘Mass Production Maker’ muttered as though he understood.

[What utter fools······.]

Apparently, some constellations had borrowed coins from the dokkaebis to join this gambling session.

Funnily enough, the ‘Brash Swamp Predator’ was among them.

[Urrrgh······ Move, move aside!]

He charged at the sparking wall to tear through the net of probability the Great Dokkaebi had spread.

Bzzzzzt!

[Gaaaaaagh!]

The Great Dokkaebi's net was powerful. The moment his hand touched the net of sparks, the ‘Brash Swamp Predator’ screamed and went sprawling. The Enforcement Division's dokkaebis rushed over and easily restrained his incarnation body.

[Let go! Let me go!]

Watching the constellations being marched away in a line, I wondered why the dokkaebis had appeared here at this precise moment.

I didn't know the exact reason, but I suspected the probability of my ‘wish right’ might have influenced it.

Dokkaebis were the species most sensitive to ‘probability’ in this world, after all.

Having restrained all the debtor constellations (債務座) in an instant, the Great Dokkaebi began transporting them through the portal.

He moved swiftly, as though he had no further business here.

Without even saying farewell, the Great Dokkaebi merely gave several constellations a warning glare before leaving.

But in the final moment before the portal closed, the Great Dokkaebi looked at me.

Beyond those cold eyes, someone was speaking to me.

「Don't make too much noise, child. The ‘King’ is watching you.」

●

After the Great Dokkaebi's visit, the Gourmet Association adjourned.

It was only natural, with the party having ended that way.

Once again, it was time for everyone to return to their own place among the stars.

Taking advantage of the confusion in the banquet hall, I quietly slipped out of the castle.

Quite a few constellations had been dragged off to the Bureau, but some who remained were still hostile toward me. I needed to leave before they started digging into my affairs.

But at the entrance to Oro Castle, I ran into a difficulty I hadn't anticipated.

······How was I supposed to get back?

There was no guide or carriage in sight.

If I lingered here, the other constellations might catch me······.

Then something came around the corner of the castle with the sound of an engine.

A luxury sports car with a sleek, streamlined body.

It reminded me of the ‘SSS-grade Ferrarghini’.

Wait. Come to think of it, the one who had made that car······.

With a screeeeech, the sports car stopped in front of me.

The window opened to reveal an old man with wispy white hair and sunglasses.

[Get in, young man.]

It was the ‘Mass Production Maker’.

●

The moment I opened the rear door, the middle of the car lengthened, transforming it into a limousine.

In sheer admiration of that strange sight, I climbed into the back seat.

Couldn't this fit any number of people?

Would he sell this car too?

I had money now, after all······.

Made of a sturdy magic-resistant material (防魔), the car was handsome inside as well as out.

I sipped iced tea from the limousine's refrigerator and examined the interior.

The ‘Mass Production Maker’ was in the driver's seat, and ‘Persephone’ sat beside him. Persephone had been communicating with someone for a while.

Perhaps it had something to do with the ‘Great Dokkaebi’ who had appeared earlier.

“Mass Production Maker, thank you for your consideration. But I have a question.”

[Hmm, what is it?]

“······Why is this woman here with us?”

I asked while glaring at ‘Anna Croft’, seated beside me.

The ‘Mass Production Maker’ grinned.

[Ho ho, I promised her a ride back. Don't fight too much, you two.]

It was deeply unpleasant, but I couldn't complain when I was getting a free ride.

I sighed and looked over my fellow passengers.

The Asgard trio rode in the back with me.

Including Iris, collapsed from exhaustion, and the unconscious Selena Kim.

While checking their condition, Anna Croft suddenly looked at me and spoke.

“······Even without your help, Iris would have survived.”

“I know. ‘Thursday's Thunder’ probably would've saved her. Isn't that right?”

At my words, Anna Croft bit her lip.

Anna Croft was crueler than anyone, but she never lost the ‘people’ who had become hers. In fact, even in a similar event she had arranged in the original novel, Anna's companions hadn't died.

Instead, that scenario had merely given them an opportunity to awaken······.

It was other people who had died.

“It was an opportunity for Iris to gain a good constellation sponsor, and you ruined everything. The future I saw was nothing like this······.”

“She wouldn't have wanted to get a sponsor that way either.”

“You're a constellation. You don't understand.”

[Tsk, tsk. I told you to get along, but apparently that won't work.]

The limousine changed shape, and my seat began moving strangely. Just as my view seemed to spin around once, I found myself in the front passenger seat where Persephone had been. The ‘Mass Production Maker’ had swapped our seats.

Apparently tired of hearing our bickering, he had even raised a soundproof partition between the front and rear seats.

The ‘Mass Production Maker’ spoke.

[You're on bad terms with incarnations as well as constellations, I see. Don't make too many enemies.]

“I don't want to make enemies either.”

In fact, I had come here to find ‘companions’, not enemies.

Though it had all ended up in this sorry state······.

The ‘Mass Production Maker’ took an electronic cigarette from inside his clothes, put it between his lips, and continued.

[I can feel anger in your Stories. Intense anger toward this world, and toward the constellations.]

I started to say something, then closed my mouth again.

[I know you saw many ugly things today.]

“It wasn't that bad.”

The ‘Mass Production Maker’ chuckled. Feeling somehow awkward, I took out my smartphone. Darkness like an unlit screen covered everything ahead of the car. As the ‘Mass Production Maker’ quietly gazed into that darkness, something like the faintest sadness flickered in his eyes.

[Still, don't hate them too much.]

Since he spoke after a brief pause, it took me a moment to understand what he meant.

[None of them could bear the loneliness, that's all. Whether a constellation is trash or noble······ they're all acting that way simply because they love ‘stories’.]

I hadn't expected the ‘Mass Production Maker’ to say something like that, and it caught me off guard. A strange sense of betrayal made my chest grow hot.

“That doesn't give them the right to trample on incarnations' lives.”

If they loved stories so much, they could undertake their own ‘scenarios’.

Instead of ruining someone else's scenario, they could move on to the next ‘scenario’.

Turning their eyes to other ‘scenarios’ and losing themselves in them was nothing more than escaping reality······.

That was what I was about to say.

But at that moment, my smartphone lit up, and ‘Ways of Survival’ came into view.

The sentences I had last been reading were displayed on the screen.

As I stared blankly at those sentences, the ‘Mass Production Maker’ asked.

[You're always looking at that. Planning to write something in that blank notepad?]

Unable to find an answer to his question, I finally gave a faint smile and replied.

“······It just calms me down when I look at it.”

In the distance, I could see the long darkness of the Dark Dimension receding.

Light was coming in through the once-empty window.

At last, it was time to return.

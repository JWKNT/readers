Protagonist

The subway train stopped about halfway across Dongho Bridge.

“My God.......”

A few survivors awkwardly got to their feet and looked outside. Seoul in ruins, and collapsed buildings.

Monsters resembling enormous snakes were tearing apart and eating the wreckage of a fighter jet that had crash-landed in the Han River.

“Wh-what the hell are those......!”

I recognized them at once.

Ichthyosaurs (魚龍).

Monsters commonly called sea serpents. They would later be classified as a grade 7 monster species in the world of ‘Ways of Survival.’

One of the ichthyosaurs turned toward us.

“A-aaah! It's coming!”

People cried out in terror. But even as I watched the ichthyosaur approach, I felt nothing. They couldn't pose a threat.

Grrrrrrr.

With a rumbling cry, the ichthyosaur circled beneath Dongho Bridge, then disappeared in a trail of bubbles.

In the world of Ways of Survival, ‘scenarios’ take precedence over everything.

So as long as we were under a scenario's protection, we wouldn't have to confront them right away. For now, at least.

[Reward settlement has been delayed by an unexpected scenario inspection. Please wait.]

Reward settlement should have begun by now, but the only thing floating in the air was an error message.

This was probably because of me.

I looked down at Kim Namwoon's corpse, of which only the torso remained.

If things had followed the original course of ‘Ways of Survival,’ Kim Namwoon should have killed most of the people in this carriage and moved on to the next scenario.

But I had prevented that.

If I was right, those enraged by Kim Namwoon's death would make themselves known about now. Here? No, not here. Up in that sky, to be precise.

[Two constellations express faint hostility toward you over the death of the character ‘Kim Namwoon.’]

Constellations (星座).

The most mysterious beings in the world of ‘Ways of Survival,’ and the forces behind the tragedy, seated atop distant nebulae (星雲), watching this entire story unfold.

Seeing the constellations' expressions of approval and disapproval (好惡) appear made it sink in that things were beginning in earnest.

It was funny.

Until just yesterday, our positions had been the exact opposite. Now they were watching me.

[A few constellations express admiration for your scenario.]

[The constellations donate 500 coins to you.]

If some of them disliked me, others were bound to like me.

Either way, the situation was equally uncomfortable, but there was nothing I could do about them for now.

I was the clown now.

As I picked up Kim Namwoon's Swiss Army knife from the floor, I thought.

Watch all you like. The price of admission will be your lives in the end.

“......Kim Dokja-ssi? Are you all right?”

I raised my head and saw Yoo Sangah's face. Drooping shoulders. Bloodstains scattered across her white blouse, and runs in her stockings. The Yoo Sangah I had known was gone. I took her hand and stood up, saying,

“I'm sorry. I couldn't save the old woman.”

I looked down at the old woman's corpse, which was also missing its head. An old woman whose name I didn't even know. Many more people would die like this.

Yoo Sangah looked at me with conflicting emotions in her eyes.

“Dokja-ssi, how can you be so.......”

“Pardon?”

“N-no, it's nothing. More importantly...... thank you.”

“What for?”

“Well, earlier.......”

A scene belatedly came to mind.

Yoo Sangah, who had happened to be standing in the direction I threw the cage. I thought I knew what she was thinking.

“It was just a coincidence. So it won't happen a second time.”

“Oh.......”

Yoo Sangah, nodding silently.

I couldn't tell what she was really thinking, but she was an intelligent woman, so she would have understood what I meant.

Because of my choice, someone had lived, and someone else had died.

Whoever the survivors were, I didn't deserve their thanks.

[Wow. Incredible.]

The dokkaebi appeared in midair with a crackle of static.

[What on earth happened here? I only stepped away to look at another carriage.......]

Delight and astonishment mingled on the dokkaebi's face.

Things resembling twinkling stars floated above its head.

I counted the stars to myself. One, two, three...... twenty, twenty-one. Twenty-one in all, then. No wonder it was pleased.

[A full twenty-one of you have joined my channel...... Haha, is this going to be my lucky day? Oh my, thank you for the donations, constellation-nims. Haha, everyone! Looks like you've earned your keep!]

The number of stars represented the number of constellations connected to the channel.

Twenty-one wasn't a large number, but it would be an unfamiliar one for a novice dokkaebi like this.

[Well, well, quite a few survivors, too! That guy in the next carriage was a lunatic as well...... You're making all sorts of interesting things happen today.]

[Survivors of carriage 3807, Bulgwang-bound train 3434: Kim Dokja, Lee Hyunsung, Yoo Sangah, Han Myungoh, Lee Gilyoung. Total survivors: 5.]

5 people. More had survived than I expected.

I looked at the survivors' faces one by one.

I had expected the well-built, athletic Lee Hyunsung to survive. To some extent, I had expected Yoo Sangah to survive, too.

And Lee Gilyoung.

If my guess was right, ‘Lee Gilyoung’ would be the name of the boy standing beside me.

The boy's hand was smeared with fluid from a crushed grasshopper.

The grasshopper I had placed in his hand.

The boy, silently looking at his mother, whose head was gone.

His mother had abandoned him and joined in killing the old woman. And this boy had watched it all, from beginning to end.

After hesitating for a moment, I put a hand on his shoulder. It wasn't some pathetic act of sympathy.

If I had to put it into words, this was.......

Yes. Hypocrisy.

“Kid.”

An unavoidable instinct.

“Do you want to live?”

“Then come with me.”

Department Head Han Myungoh.

Yoo Sangah was quite lucky, too.

Of course, that only applied to the early stages.

This constellation usually sponsored people with the ‘Chuunibyou’ attribute...... I didn't know why it had chosen me.

Then you added the words ‘fire’ and ‘judgment.’

One who was not a demon, yet passed judgment through fire.

If I remembered correctly, it was probably ‘Archangel Uriel’...... I remembered a character in the story choosing this constellation as their sponsor.

It was quite a good option, but I'd put this one on hold for now, too.

The formidable power of constellations aligned with Absolute Good (絕對善) came with absurd restrictions.

Third, ‘Secretive Plotter.’

Even I, an avid reader of Ways of Survival, had never seen this option before.

This might have been one of the constellations mentioned in passing...... but I couldn't tell at the moment. I felt I might get a sense of its identity if I read ‘Ways of Survival’ more closely.

One thing was certain: the being behind this constellation probably wasn't particularly powerful.

Even setting aside the weak adjective substituting for its true name, there wasn't a single proper noun.

Secretive Plotter—far too modest a modifier to represent a constellation. So this one went on hold, too.

Finally, ‘Prisoner of the Golden Headband.’

My heart pounded the moment I saw the fourth option.

To receive an offer from this constellation so early.

It was unmistakably ‘Prisoner of the Golden Headband.’

At first glance, the word ‘prisoner’ gave this constellation a negative image, but the proper noun ‘Golden Headband’ was what you really needed to pay attention to.

The Golden Headband (緊箍兒).

The smallest prison in the world.

Anyone who had enjoyed reading Journey to the West as a child could easily recognize the clue.

The lord of Mount Huaguo, living in the agony of a shackle that tightened around his head. The Handsome Monkey King with Fiery Golden Eyes (火眼金睛).

The Great Sage, Heaven's Equal (齊天大聖), Sun Wukong.

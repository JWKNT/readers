Omniscient Sunfish

A little later, we were heading toward the entrance to the ‘hidden dungeon’ on basement level 1. With Lee Jihye and Jung Heewon in front, I walked at the rear of the group with Lee Gilyoung, looking at my smartphone.

「······Amid pain that felt as though his head would split open, Yoo Joonghyuk’s mind gave way.

‘I give up on this life.’

That was Yoo Joonghyuk’s eighth life.」

Surely not.

It couldn’t have come to that yet.

······Damn it, why was a bastard only on his third round getting so carried away? If he’d acted cautiously like he had in his second round, he could have muddled through to the middle and later scenarios just fine.

When I looked up, Jung Heewon was watching me.

“Dokja-ssi, what are you looking at so intently?”

“···Oh, just the calendar······. With everything like this, I’ve lost track of the date.”

Looking at an actual calendar would probably be more entertaining. Sometimes I wondered how I’d managed to read this entire novel. Jung Heewon eyed me suspiciously, then turned toward Lee Jihye.

“By the way······ Jihye, was it? Are you any good with a sword, too?”

“Yes. I like swords.”

“Right? Swords really are the best. There’s something satisfying about cutting things, you know?”

“···So you know that feeling too, unnie?”

Jung Heewon smiled, then quietly studied Lee Jihye’s longsword. Its rich sheen made it obvious at a glance that it was a fine blade. Yoo Joonghyuk had probably given it to her.

“Your sword looks nice.”

“Oh, my master gave it to me. What about yours, unnie······?”

“Mine······ M-my sword’s nice, too.”

Looking down at her horn blade, which seemed shabby by comparison, Jung Heewon quietly concealed it at the other side of her waist.

I hadn’t done anything wrong, but somehow I felt guilty.

I picked on Lee Jihye for no particular reason.

“Hey, why do you speak politely to Heewon-ssi but casually to me?”

“Uh······ Well, I’ve got a bit of a weakness for cool unnies.”

When Lee Jihye answered awkwardly, Jung Heewon put her in a headlock as though she found her adorable. Apparently, those [Demon Slaying] types were on the same wavelength. After barely escaping the headlock, Lee Jihye asked,

“But why are you trying to save my master, ajusshi?”

“He’s my companion.”

“Cut the bullshit.”

“Because he’s useful.”

“······Somehow, that sounds like something my master would say.”

[The constellation ‘Secretive Plotter’ is curious about your intentions.]

Thinking about it, my actions could well have puzzled the constellations as well as Lee Jihye. Even now, the bastard was thinking about killing me whenever he got the chance, so rushing off to save him would certainly look strange.

[The constellation ‘Demon-like Judge of Fire’ is pleased by your desire to reform a fallen friend.]

[You have received a donation of 100 coins.]

And there was someone drawing their own mistaken conclusions.

But contrary to the expectations of the Demon-like Judge of Fire··· in other words, the archangel Uriel, my reason for going to save Yoo Joonghyuk was entirely personal.

Namely, to prevent his ‘regression upon death.’

Regression upon death. It sounded great. The stigma ‘Regression,’ which returned him to the past every time he died. Befitting a protagonist, it was an undeniably overpowered ability.

The problem, however, was that this ability stirred up some rather complicated thoughts in the supporting characters around him.

「But what happens to the world after you regress?」

At some point after Yoo Joonghyuk’s life count reached double digits, a supporting character had asked him that question. I’d forgotten the name, but Yoo Joonghyuk’s answer remained vivid.

「···I don’t know, either. I simply always choose a world where more people can live.」

It sounded convincing, but what he really meant was that an abandoned world was none of his business. In fact, nowhere in ‘Ways of Survival’ was there any definite theoretical explanation of what happened to a world after he regressed.

Scientific, magical, or otherwise.

That was precisely why I was uneasy.

What happened to a world after its regressor disappeared?

Did it reset along with the regressor?

Or did it split off into another branch of a parallel universe?

The latter would be a relief, but if it was the former, then my existence······.

“Hyung?”

“Oh, huh?”

Lee Gilyoung, who was holding on to my clothes beside me, was looking up at me with worried eyes.

“I think we’re here?”

[You are approaching an area outside the permitted zone. Take care not to leave the scenario area.]

A warning message popping up. It didn’t matter. Chungmuro’s ‘hidden dungeon’ was treated as an area ‘within the permitted zone’ anyway.

Exit 1, coming into view as we rounded the corner. Beside it, the dungeon entrance greeted us, shimmering with ominous shadows.

[You have discovered a ‘hidden dungeon’!]

[This dungeon has already been discovered by someone else. You cannot obtain the first discovery achievement.]

[A new hidden scenario has arrived!]

●

<Hidden Scenario ― Theatre Dungeon>

Category : Hidden

Difficulty : A-

Clear Conditions : Defeat the master of the Theatre Dungeon.

Time Limit : None

Reward : 4000 coins

Upon Failure : ―

●

Startled, Lee Jihye hesitantly stepped back.

“······What’s this? Theatre Dungeon?”

Lee Gilyoung looked startled, too. Well, this was probably the first time any of them had received a ‘hidden scenario.’ Jung Heewon also chimed in.

“A movie theatre turned into a dungeon······ There’s something romantic about that.”

Romantic, huh. She could only say that because she didn’t know how frightening a movie theatre could be. We proceeded inside the theatre. The familiar basement lobby of a multiplex greeted us.

[You have entered the ‘Theatre Dungeon’!]

Despite the tension with which we’d entered, the dungeon’s interior was desolate. A multiplex with nine floors in total, from basement level 1 to floor 8.

“Hyung, all the posters are torn. Who did that?”

“Who knows?”

That was what I said, but I actually knew the reason.

The key to this ‘Theatre Dungeon’ lay in the ‘posters’ on the walls. Yoo Joonghyuk had probably advanced upstairs while clearing all the trials contained in each poster. No doubt he’d intended to sweep up every trial reward.

Apart from the torn posters, there was nothing particularly strange about basement level 1. No items, no monsters. The only unusual thing was an elevator in one corner, smashed, its doors warped.

Lee Jihye asked,

“Is this really a dungeon? Why isn’t there anything here?”

“Something will turn up once we go upstairs.”

“···Do you know something, ajusshi?”

“A little.”

“How? There’s something suspicious about you, ajusshi. Are you on your second life or something?”

That would be your master.

No, that bastard was on his third.

Then Jung Heewon said,

“It’s because Dokja-ssi’s sponsor is Gung Ye.”

“···Really?”

Ignoring the two women’s banter, I was about to head to the ground floor when Lee Gilyoung suddenly grabbed me. The cockroach on his head was waving its antennae violently. Lee Jihye drew her sword almost at the same instant that I raised a hand to my mouth.

“Shh. There’s someone here besides us.”

Once we quieted our breathing, faint sounds of movement began to reach us. Directly upstairs. So······ near the lobby? At first I thought it might be Yoo Joonghyuk, but it wasn’t his voice.

“···So you’re sure, right? That there’s······ loads of that stuff here.”

“I told you, yes. I paid them a whole 1000 coins for this information.”

“You mean the <Prophets>?”

“Yeah. They’re a little creepy, but their information’s solid.”

Their murmured conversation drifted toward us. We climbed the escalator one step at a time, approaching them. Four men were gathered in the ground-floor lobby.

Lee Jihye whispered in my ear.

“Who the hell are those guys? I’ve never seen any of them in Chungmuro.”

“They probably came through the entrance aboveground.”

“Aboveground? Isn’t that covered in deadly poisonous fog? And the scenario, too―”

“Each station has different scenarios, and they progress at different speeds. They probably finished theirs faster than our station did. Eating subterranean species’ meat takes care of mild poisoning.”

Despite what I said, I was just as bewildered.

The <Prophets>?

There had been no information about people like that in any of Yoo Joonghyuk’s lives so far. At this point, Yoo Joonghyuk and I were the only ones who knew about the hidden dungeon in the first place.

What new variable had appeared?

It seemed I needed to find out.

“Then let’s go in.”

As the men spoke, a blue spotlight switched on in midair. The bright light seemed to envelop them, and then they vanished from sight.

“···What just happened?”

Jung Heewon asked, but I didn’t answer. Instead, I examined the posters on the wall. This one was torn, and that one, too······ Only when I reached the end of the wall did I find the sole intact poster. I read the words printed on it.

Steven Spielberg, Samuel L. Jackson, Jeff Goldblum······.

That bastard Yoo Joonghyuk··· Of all things, he’d left only this one? Just what I’d expect of his third round.

At that moment, the light returned.

With a whirrrrr― the spotlight aimed at us. Alarmed, Lee Jihye and Lee Gilyoung recoiled, but there was no way to avoid it. This beam was designed to hit us.

At the last moment, I asked Jung Heewon,

“Heewon-ssi, do you like movies?”

“Of course. Doesn’t pretty much everyone?”

“Then you might start hating them now.”

“What’s this all of a sudden―”

[You have been struck by a projection beam.]

[The screening on this floor will begin.]

The scenery around us slowly began to change. Perhaps because this wasn’t a mere illusion, [Fourth Wall] didn’t work as it had before. The worn linoleum floor turned into green undergrowth, while the information desk and the concession stand where popcorn had been popped became dense rainforest. The ceiling had transformed into an endless blue sky without a single cloud. Flustered, Lee Jihye muttered,

“Where the hell are we?”

Shouting, Lee Jihye slashed wildly at the surrounding trees and bushes, but nothing changed. With a calm expression, Lee Gilyoung began searching for insects.

I touched a nearby tree to test it. A hard, damp texture. It was a real tropical rainforest recreating the Mesozoic Era. The realism was on an entirely different level from the Specter’s [Phantom Prison]. As expected, this was probably the power of the theatre master on the dungeon’s eighth floor.

“We’re inside a movie.”

“······It really is one impossible thing after another.”

A novel had become reality, so there was no rule saying a movie couldn’t become reality, too.

Quick to adapt, Jung Heewon seemed to accept it right away.

“Ajusshi, what movie is this?”

“You’ll find out soon.”

“···Can’t you just tell me? Wait, kid, what are you···”

At that moment, the bushes stirred, and something sprang out in front of Lee Gilyoung. It was an insect resembling a giant praying mantis. About 40 cm long, perhaps. Horrified, Lee Jihye adjusted her grip on her longsword and shouted,

“Hey, kid! Get back, quick!”

But Lee Gilyoung’s expression seemed to ask what all the fuss was about.

“It’s not a praying mantis. It’s an insect from the Triassic called Titanoptera.”

“What?”

Lee Gilyoung reached straight toward the insect called Titano-whatever. It didn’t reject his touch, and a little later, both Lee Gilyoung and the insect were enveloped in blue light.

Lee Jihye looked at me blankly.

“What······ is he?”

“Fabre.”

Bringing Lee Gilyoung had definitely been a good decision. With his ability, we might be able to get through the trials more easily than I’d expected.

The giant mantis twitched its large mouth, and Lee Gilyoung nodded repeatedly. I wasn’t sure, but presumably some sort of conversation was taking place.

A little later, however, Lee Gilyoung’s face began to pale as he talked to the mantis.

······What was wrong?

Lee Gilyoung hurriedly turned toward me.

“Hyung!”

No sooner had he spoken than an earthshaking rumble sounded. Something was charging toward us at tremendous speed, smashing huge palm trees to pieces.

GROOOOOAR!

The muzzle of the enormous reptile emerging through the rainforest was stained with bright red blood. In front of it, several bloodied men were running toward us. They were the men who’d entered before us.

“Aaaaaaagh!”

“H-help me!”

Backing away hesitantly, Lee Jihye spoke to Jung Heewon.

“I know what movie this is.”

“······Yeah, me too.”

A height well over ten metres, and a tough hide. Brutal muscle covering its entire body. The Mesozoic Era’s strongest predator stood before our eyes.

A beast comparable to a grade-7 monster species, even at a glance.

Considering that this was still only the dungeon’s first floor, the difficulty was atrocious, but instead my heart began to race.

Because the harder a ‘hidden dungeon’ was, the better its rewards.

I drew my sword and said,

“Get ready to fight.”

Yoo Joonghyuk had probably skipped this movie after looking only at its subject matter. The Theatre Dungeon’s main rewards were related to the subject of each movie. He must have thought there was no way a movie full of dinosaurs would offer any useful rewards. But he wouldn’t know.

That this movie, too, held a truly important reward.

“···Seriously? You’re going to fight that thing?”

“We have to kill it to make an exit.”

“An exit?”

“We’re inside a movie. Did you forget?”

The T-Rex’s massive body, approaching in great strides. Behind it, I could see the island’s central research laboratory. And the escape helicopter stationed on the laboratory roof.

We were inside a movie. A movie made tangible by the master of the ‘Theatre Dungeon.’

So there was only one way to escape.

“Let’s give it a great ending.”

The moment its vivid yellow eyes turned toward us, an ear-splitting roar rang out.

GROOOOOAR!

[The grade-7 earth dragon species ‘Tyrannosaurus Rex’ has noticed you.]

[‘Tyrannosaurus Rex’ has activated the skill ‘Predation Fear’!]

[The exclusive skill ‘Fourth Wall’ blocks the effects of ‘Predation Fear.’]

Thanks to [Fourth Wall], my mind was calm, but it couldn’t keep the hairs on my body from standing on end. This must have been the fear felt by prey.

“Everyone, dodge!”

Jung Heewon and Lee Jihye, who’d briefly frozen, snapped out of it. I picked up Lee Gilyoung, who was right beside me, and ran toward the rear.

CRASH, CRASH, CRASH, CRASH!

An enormous tail came sweeping in, destroying every tree in front of it.

“Guuurgh!”

The fleeing men were struck in the back and sent flying, vomiting blood. Fortunately, Jung Heewon and Lee Jihye seemed to have barely escaped the danger zone.

I put Lee Gilyoung down and shouted,

“Gilyoung, fall back! Heewon-ssi and Jihye, spread out to the left and right!”

That was when a message appeared beside me.

[The character ‘Lee Gilyoung’ activates the skill ‘Dinosaur Book’!]

···Huh?

“Tyrannosaurus is agile for its size, but its narrow field of vision makes it vulnerable to attacks from its blind spots.”

“···What?”

“I read it in a dinosaur book when I was little.”

“When you were little?”

“···When I was younger than I am now.”

I stared blankly at Lee Gilyoung, then shut my mouth.

Right, this wasn’t the time for pointless quibbling.

GROOOOAR!

[You activate the exclusive skill ‘White Pure Star Energy’!]

Waving my brilliant blade from side to side, I began drawing the T-Rex’s attention. Neither Lee Jihye nor Jung Heewon was a tank. Lee Gilyoung wasn’t either, of course. That left me as the only one who could take the risk here.

“While I keep its attention, get behind it―”

Before I’d even finished speaking, Lee Jihye and Jung Heewon were already sprinting behind the T-Rex. It was nice that they caught on quickly.

KROOOOAR!

I barely dodged the teeth of the charging beast before its hind foot came flying at me. Before I could swing [Unbreakable Faith], its tail followed, grazing just above my hair. A chilling shudder swept through my body. My Constitution level had passed 20, so getting hit by that wouldn’t kill me, but it was still terrifying.

Perhaps I was the one who’d been lucky all this time.

Perhaps the ‘sunfish’ who’d die at the slightest brush was actually me, rather than Yoo Joonghyuk.

Slash! Swish!

Meanwhile, Jung Heewon and Lee Jihye were steadily dealing damage from behind it. A combination of [Sword Training] and [Kendo]. Brilliant sword strikes were adding more and more wounds to the T-Rex’s thick legs.

If they activated [Demon Slaying] as well, killing the T-Rex would take a little time, but shouldn’t be too difficult.

“Hyung! I’ll draw its attention!”

This little brat. I’d told him to stay back, but he wouldn’t listen.

“No, Gilyoung, you―”

“I can do it!”

Suddenly, Lee Gilyoung stepped forward and began making incomprehensible hand signals. I was wondering what that was about when the giant mantis flew in from somewhere, jabbed the T-Rex’s eye, and fled. It was that Titano-whatever insect from earlier.

GROOOAR!

The T-Rex’s eyes darted in confusion as the mantis disrupted its vision. Judging by the elaborate hand movements, Lee Gilyoung seemed to be controlling the mantis. I looked down at him with renewed interest.

Between this and that Dinosaur Book skill earlier, wasn’t this kid actually an incredibly overpowered character?

No wonder Yoo Joonghyuk wanted him.

Krrr, GROOOOAR!

Thanks to Lee Gilyoung’s outstanding performance, the battle turned in our favour in an instant. The T-Rex’s movements had grown much slower, and before I knew it, a red murderous gleam shone in Jung Heewon’s and Lee Jihye’s eyes.

[Demon Slaying].

It had the drawback of leaving its user vulnerable to mental attacks, but it was a good skill that increased combat power with heightened excitement. The sight of the two women darting through the rainforest with blazing eyes was truly spectacular.

It stung quite a bit to see this after losing Lee Jihye to Yoo Joonghyuk.

Still, Jung Heewon was overwhelmingly superior in potential for growth. ‘Judge of Evil’ was that good an attribute, and Jung Heewon hadn’t even made a <Sponsorship Contract> yet.

The T-Rex seemed to have lost quite a bit of health by now. Time to land the final blow?

[You activate ‘Blade of Faith’!]

I began concentrating my remaining magic power into the blade. I had no constellation sponsor, and I wasn’t as nimble as Jung Heewon or Lee Jihye. But that didn’t mean my combat power was weak. I had an overpowered item that made up for all of it.

WOOOOOSH!

Ether Blade.

[The special option of ‘Unbreakable Faith’ activates.]

[The ether’s element changes to ‘Flame.’]

An enormous amount of magic power drained out all at once, leaving my body severely fatigued. But I had a way to replenish it later, so I didn’t worry.

In an instant, the blade lengthened by over 1 metre, and flames coiled around it.

I ran behind the tyrannosaurus.

“Everyone, get out of the way!”

The moment the sluggish tyrannosaurus faltered, I climbed up its tail. Without the [Sense of Balance] skill, I nearly fell several times, but by repeatedly driving my blade into its skin, I somehow managed to avoid falling off.

KROOOOAR!

Bleeding all over, the T-Rex rolled on the ground. I drove my blade in wherever I could. Flames rose from each wound when I pulled the sword out.

Gasping in agony, the T-Rex glared at me one final time with its yellow eyes before they rolled back.

[You have become the first to successfully hunt the grade-7 earth dragon species ‘Tyrannosaurus Rex’!]

[You have obtained 1000 coins as a reward.]

“Ah······ We really killed it.”

“I told you I could do it.”

At my words, the panting Jung Heewon looked proud. A T-Rex ranked among the strongest grade-7 monster species, so she had every right to be. Lee Jihye ran over a little later and grumbled at me.

“I’d practically killed it already!”

“As if. It would’ve taken a lot more hits to die.”

For no particular reason, I made a show of taking the credit as I wiped my sword. Jung Heewon asked,

“But did they kill the tyrannosaurus in this movie?”

“No, but isn’t this more entertaining?”

“···Pardon?”

“If it’s a fantasy action adventure, we ought to do at least this much.”

At that moment, a system message appeared in my mind.

[The theatre master is satisfied with the movie’s altered ending.]

Jung Heewon cried out in disbelief.

“Huh?”

That was right. The way to clear the ‘Theatre Dungeon’ wasn’t to see the ‘real ending.’

If it were, even Yoo Joonghyuk wouldn’t have cleared this dungeon. The key was to create the ending the ‘theatre master’ wanted. And for the record, this theatre master was an extreme catharsis junkie.

“You get it now, right? We just have to smash everything.”

In other words, if we removed every obstacle to the ending, the movie would naturally come to a conclusion.

[You can now move to the next floor.]

[Proceed to the helipad at the top of the laboratory.]

“Let’s move in a little while. We need to collect some rewards, too.”

With that, I began looking around the T-Rex. Before long, I found one of the men who’d entered before us. The others seemed to have been eaten by the T-Rex or torn to death.

“Hey, stay with me.”

“U-uuurgh······.”

Blood was still pouring from the man’s back. A wound from the T-Rex’s claws. Judging by the fully exposed bones, he was already beyond saving.

“Breathe out slowly.”

“C-cough! ······Save······.”

“How did you find out about this place?”

“P-proph······.”

“Who exactly are the <Prophets>?”

The man’s breathing was growing even harsher.

“Those······ who received······ r-revelations······.”

······Revelations?

“I want······ to live······.”

“That man······?”

I shook my head, looking down at the fallen man.

‘Revelations,’ huh. Interesting nonsense.

Because the only similar ability in the ‘Ways of Survival’ I knew was ‘Future Sight,’ and the only person with that ability was the Prophet ‘Anna Croft.’

Then there was only one answer.

······So there were others besides me.

But they didn’t know as much as I did.

The proof was that they’d leaked information about this place to test it rather than coming themselves.

“Let’s rest a little before we go.”

We covered the man’s body with large leaves and gathered near the dead T-Rex. We needed to hurry to catch up with Yoo Joonghyuk, but if we chased him without enough rest, the entire group might be wiped out before we even met him.

I searched the T-Rex’s carcass.

“···Do you think we can eat this, too?”

“New meat!”

“Ah, this is the tastiest thing in the world······.”

“Whew······ That was a good meal. It’s so delicious, I could cry because I can’t eat any more.”

[Constitution Enhancement Ampoule]

[Magic Power Enhancement Ampoule]

[Agility Enhancement Ampoule]

[Strength Enhancement Ampoule]

“Ajusshi, what are you collecting?”

······That uncannily sharp girl.

“What’s this, a Constitution Enhancement Ampoule?”

“You weren’t planning to take them all yourself, were you?”

“···It is a hidden scenario.”

Damn, this was awkward. I hadn’t killed the T-Rex by myself, so taking everything would prick my conscience, but it felt like a waste to share······.

This sly little brat was bullshitting me······.

“Hm? Heewon-unnie, can’t I have those?”

Heart of Steel

We returned straight to Seoul.

The first thing we checked was the [Industrial Complex]’s safety.

“No further unusual signs have been detected since then.”

Aileen, who was in charge of the central situation room, spoke.

Footage from around the Pacific was being broadcast on the situation room’s panels.

An enormous island that had risen in the Pacific beside the American continent. It was unmistakably one of the ‘disasters’ I knew.

In the main story of Ways of Survival, that island’s master had been a powerful Outer God. The question was whether it would be the same being this time.

“Where’s my mother?”

“On the east coast.”

“…The east coast?”

[The forgotten island is beginning to rise!]

Along with the system message, I saw waves surging from across the distant Pacific. Crustal movements on that scale would have sent immense tsunamis toward shores all over the world. Of course, the United States had suffered the most.

—Krrrraaack!

Amid the incarnations’ agonized screams, New York’s urban districts were being swept away. The tsunami wasn’t all. Beneath it swarmed the Nameless Ones, lower entities among the Outer Gods. These lower entities among the Outer Gods flooded in indiscriminately, gnawing away at the entire American mainland.

There was no time even to ask for aid. Less than thirty minutes after the disaster began, half the mainland had vanished. One hour into the disaster, the United States was covered in a pitch-black aura.

It was a disaster on a scale incomparable to the [Dark Castle] or the [Disaster of Floods] that had appeared in Seoul.

“Don’t tell me my mother…”

Aileen nodded and added,

“I’m not sure whether I should call it… fortunate.”

The next moment, the panel switched to the Korean Peninsula’s east coast. As expected, the tsunami had reached us too. The waves were lower than those that had struck the American mainland, and there were no Nameless Ones in sight, but the tsunami itself was still an enormous natural disaster.

—Heavenly Wind God!

At my mother’s cry, powerful winds burst from the folding fan at her fingertips. Pungbaek, who had been so obstinate during the Great War of Saints and Demons, seemed to be helping properly this time.

Cho Youngran, who could be called my mother’s right hand, was also fighting hard.

[The Giant Story ‘Shindansu’ begins its story!]

<Hongik> seemed to be watching the crisis too. Rooted in the sea, [Shindansu]’s Story was using the Korean Peninsula’s probability to hold back the disaster.

As though recovering from a terrible fright, Han Sooyoung muttered,

“…Wait, America. Isn’t that where that woman is?”

There was only one person Han Sooyoung would refer to as ‘that woman.’

“Couldn’t she predict this with Future Sight?”

“Actually, there’s something I need to tell you about that…”

Before Aileen could finish, a door on one side of the situation room opened and someone entered. The moment Yoo Joonghyuk recognized her, his hand went to the [Black Heavenly Demon Sword].

“I’m not here to fight, so put your sword away, Supreme King.”

The [Great Demon’s Eyes], pulsing with brilliant light.

The Prophet Anna Croft and Zarathustra, the group she led, stood there.

The aftereffects of Steel Transformation Stage 5 seemed more serious than I had thought.

Staying in Seoul didn’t make their duties any easier.

And so the ‘Demon-like Judge of Fire’ became ‘The Living Flame.’ An archangel who had become an ‘Outer God’ solely for her own revenge. That was why she was here now.

【‘Yoo Joonghyuk’ does not die. He merely regresses.】

【Shut up! With words like that—】

Deflecting flames stained with deep fury, Yoo Joonghyuk kept speaking.

【He awoke and lived the 1000th round. He died, and lived again in the 1001st round. He lived, and lived, and survived again.】

I knew that life too.

A life no one remembered, in which no one could accompany him.

Yoo Joonghyuk had kept living that life alone.

【And so he became me.】

Uriel charged as though she had heard something she must never hear. Her wildly swinging [Flames of Retribution] cut Yoo Joonghyuk’s waist and shoulder. Wheeling around in an instant, the blade next aimed for his neck. As though it were a punishment he deserved to bear, Yoo Joonghyuk did not block it. And

as if by magic, Uriel’s sword strike stopped.

【You, you, you…】

Perhaps Uriel knew too. That she could never have her revenge.

Because the being who had taken her dearest comrade away was that comrade himself.

Yoo Joonghyuk spoke.

【Kill me if you wish. I am the ‘Secretive Plotter’ who took your worldline from you.】

Uriel let out a terrible shriek, then a wailing roar.

The very moment her sword moved again, seawater exploded with a great “Boom!”

Uriel’s sword flew through the air. With a thud, her infernal flame plunged into the sea and sank, vaporizing the water.

Yoo Joonghyuk hadn’t done it.

Fwoooooosh…

Cannon smoke rising from beyond the tidal wave. It was a shell fired by the Lee Jihye of the 999th round.

【That’s enough, Uriel.】

Lee Jihye’s voice was steeped in joy and madness. She continued.

【Yes, I knew. I knew it all…】

Staggering, the Lee Jihye of the 999th round walked across the sea toward us.

Yoo Joonghyuk did not evade her pale hand.

【Captain. You’re in there, aren’t you? You’re something else now, but you’ve been in there all along, haven’t you? Right? You were alive after all, weren’t you?】

Chaos poured from Lee Jihye’s eyes instead of tears. A fine powder, as though made from pitch-black darkness. Yoo Joonghyuk nodded at her.

[‘Yoo Joonghyuk of the 999th round’ looks at his old companion.]

Gripping Yoo Joonghyuk’s collar, the Lee Jihye of the 999th round slowly crumpled.

I looked at Yoo Joonghyuk’s back. A back from which I could read no expression.

「It was a trick of the worldlines that had created multiple Yoo Joonghyuks.」

The Yoo Joonghyuk who lived the zeroth round became the Yoo Joonghyuk of the first, then the second. The Yoo Joonghyuk of the second became the third, and the third became the fourth.

With so many abnormal events in which past and future interfered with each other, I had briefly forgotten that obvious fact. But it was the truth.

「A regressor does not actually regress. What regresses is not him, but everything except him.」

Even when other people’s time turned backward, his time had always moved forward.

Though the worldlines had split, making one the Yoo Joonghyuk of the 1864th round and another ‘Secretive Plotter’—

「From the beginning, he had been ‘one person,’ walking a continuous path.」

But could they really bear that truth?

「Someone had lived to avenge him.」

Uriel, burning with flame.

「Someone had lived to carry his wishes forward.」

Lee Hyunsung, who could no longer cry.

「Someone had lived to fight him once more.」

Kim Namwoon, standing crookedly in the air and glaring at us.

「Someone had lived to bring back every moment spent with him.」

Lee Jihye, crumpled in bewilderment before us.

Yoo Joonghyuk had said it. ‘Regression changes nothing.’

But his regressions had changed someone’s life.

To these people, ‘Yoo Joonghyuk’ was a world.

A world that had kept them living even after their own worldline was destroyed.

「Kim Dokja’s plan lay in that very ‘world.’」

If they still remembered their world.

If, because of that, they accepted this ‘Yoo Joonghyuk’ as their captain again.

「Then perhaps this fight would no longer need to continue.」

【If you really are the Captain…you must know what I want too.】

The Lee Jihye of the 999th round smiled brightly.

【Let’s go back, Captain. Let’s all start again.】

Clutching Yoo Joonghyuk’s wrist, she spoke.

【Let’s destroy this worldline together. All right? I even made a deal with the Dokkaebi King. If we just destroy this worldline, he’ll let us go back to that time. He’ll contact your sponsor, the ‘Oldest Dream’—】

I hastily looked at Yoo Joonghyuk.

—Yoo Joonghyuk.

We couldn’t provoke them here. We had to sweet-talk them as much as we could. Even if we had to pretend to agree with her, right now—

“Lee Jihye.”

Yoo Joonghyuk spoke as he looked at Lee Jihye. In his own ordinary voice, not a True Voice.

Under that gaze, the Lee Jihye of the 999th round hunched her shoulders. As though it were the day she first learned swordsmanship from her master.

“Is that really what you want?”

【…】

“Do you really think everyone will be happy if we go back to that time?”

【The Captain I know…doesn’t talk like that.】

Biting her lip, the Lee Jihye of the 999th round let go of Yoo Joonghyuk’s hand.

【He, he’s someone who regressed 999 times. Someone who never fell, even in the face of all those countless years. He would never say something so weak—】

“Even a person who has regressed 999 times can grow tired on the thousandth.”

Yoo Joonghyuk was speaking. In a voice so honest that even I was startled.

“Even after enduring 1,000 lives, he may give up in his 1001st life.”

Even I was left at a loss by the immeasurable weariness in his voice.

【That can’t… That can’t be. The Captain I know—】

“Doesn’t give up. But if that’s all you remember of ‘Yoo Joonghyuk’—”

I had to tell him. That he couldn’t speak like that now.

But I couldn’t say it.

“That Yoo Joonghyuk is dead.”

Those were Yoo Joonghyuk’s true feelings. The inner self of a person who had lived 1,864 lives, which he had never once laid bare.

Lee Jihye cried out, almost screaming in anguish.

【That can’t be. That can’t be!】

“He will no longer regress.”

Something glittered in the distant sky. Stars.

[The constellation ‘Demon-like Judge of Fire’ looks around with an urgent expression!]

[The constellation ‘Most Ancient Liberator’ asks after the youngest’s safety!]

[The constellation ‘Abyssal Black Flame Dragon’ declares that this time, he will use both hands!]

The stars who had watched our story for so long were coming toward us. And beyond those stars, our companions were rushing over.

Han Sooyoung, Yoo Sangah, Jung Heewon…the people of <Kim Dokja’s Company> who had lived in this world with us.

In the darkness of the setting sun, they looked like a single enormous constellation.

Taking in that entire scene, that one person—Yoo Joonghyuk—spoke.

“I cannot go back. This is my final round.”

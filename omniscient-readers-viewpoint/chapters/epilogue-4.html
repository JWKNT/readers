“This world no longer needs the system. Why did you bring those Stories back?”

One reason they had attempted [Group Regression] was that Kim Dokja's avatar was weakening. Han Sooyoung had brought Bureau Stories back in case the same thing happened to Kim Dokja.

“Why bother? As you know, he'll never wake again.”

“Kim Dokja isn't dead ye―”

“If you really think that, why are you stopping me?”

For an instant, she was speechless.

In a flash, Yoo Joonghyuk got behind her and drove his fist into her back. Han Sooyoung crashed to the ground and rose, coughing up gritty dust. Staggering, she shouted.

“······Yoo Joonghyuk, get a grip! Do you think Kim Dokja wants you doing this? Kim Dokja told us not to abandon this world. You agreed, too!”

“Perhaps I would have.”

Yoo Joonghyuk's golden eyes shone through the hazy dust. Those eyes were asking Han Sooyoung.

“Are you any different?”

Han Sooyoung couldn't answer. Instead, the Stories of Kim Dokja remaining in her hands answered.

[The Story ‘One Who Opposes the Extraordinary’ grieves.]

Words she hadn't managed to let go of. She had endured life by writing them somewhere. Don't regress. Live in the present. Repeating those trite words from the past, she had endured each moment. For two years.

“You haven't been able to forget anything, either.”

“Shut up.”

Leaping up in an instant, Han Sooyoung punched Yoo Joonghyuk in the face. Every time their fists met, the Stories they shared and had built together shook.

[The Story ‘King of Kaizenix’ wavers.]

She'd believed she'd worked hard to hold on. She'd thought a great deal of time had passed, measured by the sentences she'd written. Breathing, eating, sleeping. Han Sooyoung had survived like that.

「You don't have to regress to be a regressor.」

But could she say she had been living?

「Some people spend their entire lives in a past that has already gone.」

As the bones in their fists shattered, Stories scattered a little at a time. Raw memories preserved without a single revision. Unconsciously, Han Sooyoung gathered the scattering Stories.

Because she didn't want to lose even one. Because she didn't want to forget even one.

「For the past two years, she hadn't moved a single step.」

Gasping, Han Sooyoung spoke.

“What would doing this now change?”

“······.”

“You won't find Kim Dokja even if you leave. And you can't leave anyway.”

“······.”

“This worldline's [Ark] is smashed. Have you already forgotten what happened in the final war? That isn't an Ark. We can't leave this worldline!”

Their powers collided once more. With a rumbling crash, their magic made a storm. At its center, Yoo Joonghyuk spoke.

“I gathered so many Stories, but I still don't know what my ■■ is.”

Fierce Stories burst in the air. Paying no heed to the damage to his precious Stories, Yoo Joonghyuk swung his fist.

“You wrote my story. Then you must know where my story should end.”

For an instant, sentences flowed through Han Sooyoung's mind.

「Had Yoo Joonghyuk really come here to obtain the [Final Ark]?」

A belated realization swept over her.

The regressor exhausted by scenarios had, in truth, been able to live because the scenarios existed.

Yoo Joonghyuk gathered all his Stories. The Stories of Yoo Joonghyuk, who had finally escaped the curse of regression, now bared their hostility toward Han Sooyoung.

“Give it everything, Han Sooyoung.”

「This was Yoo Joonghyuk's last fight.」

Every cell in her body rang an alarm.

A regressor who had repeated his long life.

An emotion rose in the eyes she had described dozens, hundreds of times. Han Sooyoung knew what it was.

Yoo Joonghyuk wanted to die here.

At the hands of none other than the one who had written his first sentence.

“Fuck! You've never done what I wanted, not even once!”

Kwoooooom!

Unleashing his full power, Yoo Joonghyuk swung his fist again. A blow packed with great Stories. The final mark of emphasis in their clash was about to be made. Han Sooyoung released all her Stories, too. And.

A roar erupted as though a star had exploded.

Her whole body hurt as if it had been beaten. Every bone in her outstretched right fist shattered. Her companions' shouts and people's screams. Amid the pain tearing through her ears, Han Sooyoung endured the impact. Her body was in tatters.

Yoo Joonghyuk lay collapsed before her.

Her heart pounded.

“Yoo Joonghyuk?”

His fingertips trembled faintly. Slowly opening his eyes, Yoo Joonghyuk looked up at her. Gasping, Han Sooyoung forced out the words.

“······A little different from the Dark Castle, huh?”

As she said it, both her legs buckled. When had she been hit? The bones in her knees were completely shattered.

“Doesn't seem that different.”

“You son of a bitch······.”

They fell face down side by side onto the ground. Han Sooyoung crawled toward him. She didn't think she could calm down without hitting him once more, somehow.

Just as her shaking hand was about to smack the back of his head, Yoo Joonghyuk's equally shaking right arm caught her wrist. After struggling in the air for a long time, their arms finally fell limp across each other. Now they truly had no strength left.

Scars where their colliding Stories had torn the sky remained overhead. Through the hideously rent sky, they could see the distant 〈Star Stream〉. The few stars left in the night shone faintly toward them.

After watching that sight for a long time, Yoo Joonghyuk spoke as though in passing.

“······They said Kim Dokja scattered into the universe.”

Kim Dokja's soul, scattered into tiny pieces. How much Kim Dokja was in those little fragments? Han Sooyoung didn't know, either.

But those little Kim Dokjas would have been born as something on the edges of worldlines she didn't know. They might have been born human. It might be somewhere like Earth. This time, perhaps they were born on a continent other than the Korean Peninsula.

“Do you think he's found happiness?”

The moment she heard that, Han Sooyoung felt as though something had ended.

An awful pain seized her heart. She distinctly heard a sound like something breaking. The sound of one story ending. The sound of their long mourning finally ending. The sound of someone who had lived only in the past finally letting that past go. At that moment, Han Sooyoung was gripped by a strange sense of guilt.

“Kim Dokja······.”

「Hadn't she actually hoped he wouldn't give up?」

Even if everyone else let go of this long grief, hadn't she hoped at least one person would keep doing those damned things, ruining his own life in the process?

Listening to Yoo Joonghyuk's hacking cough, Han Sooyoung said what she had to.

“I'm sure he's doing well. He's strong.”

“······.”

“He'll be making a life for himself and living happily there, too. Maybe reading another strange book.”

“Even if we found him, he wouldn't remember anything.”

Their mourning ended there.

There was no point in crossing worldlines. Even if they found that ‘Kim Dokja,’ what could they do? They couldn't force the past onto someone who remembered nothing. A reincarnated Kim Dokja wasn't Kim Dokja. Nowhere in this universe was the Kim Dokja they knew.

And yet Han Sooyoung said something strange in that moment.

“We don't know that. If Ways of Survival exists where he was born.”

For a moment, she didn't know why she'd said it.

“I mean, Ways of Survival······.”

As though defying her will, her mouth murmured.

「Kim Dokja, who became the ‘Oldest Dream,’ scattered throughout the universe.」

Her mind spilled out confused sentences.

「This universe is sustained by the imagination of that ‘Oldest Dream.’」

「Then what is the ‘Oldest Dream’ dreaming now?」

Goosebumps rose along her forearms. It was a thought she'd tried not to have.

「“Ajusshi in another worldline would like books, too, right?”」

It really was an absurd delusion.

Yet Han Sooyoung couldn't stop thinking it.

Kim Dokja, far away on the other side of the universe, reading someone's novel with an expression unfamiliar to her.

“Would he······still be curious how this story ends?”

“······What are you talking about?”

“If the countless ‘Kim Dokjas’ scattered through the universe read a certain story at the same time······.”

「Why do constellations try to spread their Stories far and wide?」

「Why is ‘story’ the foundation of this world?」

“If all the Kim Dokjas who forgot they're the ‘Oldest Dream’ dreamed the same story.”

A way to bring Kim Dokja back without ruining the lives of the Kim Dokjas reincarnated in other worldlines.

In a hazy voice, Han Sooyoung continued.

“If the story he dreams······is the same story we want······?”

A black shadow falling over her head broke her reverie.

“We were going to build a park here, but you two have made a complete mess of it.”

How long had Anna Croft been standing there?

“Are you thinking of crossing worldlines again?”

Seeing Anna Croft's face, Han Sooyoung finally came to her senses. Only then did she realize what she'd been imagining, and she felt ashamed.

It was nonsense to begin with. Making Kim Dokjas in other worldlines dream of the novel she'd written? Nothing could be more ridiculous.

Above all, there was no longer any way to cross worldlines from this world.

But Anna Croft's expression was a little strange.

“I thought a day like this might come.”

Anna Croft's eyes shone red. Her gaze was on the museum spire.

The model of the [Final Ark].

Her heart beat slowly. It couldn't be possible. It couldn't, and yet······why?

Rrrrrumble.

Very slowly, the model above the museum began to rise into the air.

Before she knew it, Yoo Joonghyuk had raised himself up and was staring that way, his eyes wide.

An Ark. It was very small, but it was unmistakably an Ark.

“I collected parts and repaired it over twenty years, just in case. If you didn't return, I wanted to try looking for you, at least once. There weren't many usable parts, so I couldn't restore it fully······.”

“It can be used. But only one person can board it.”

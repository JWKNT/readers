Starting the day after the banquet, the group found themselves enjoying unexpected luxuries.

“……But Dokja-ssi, is it really all right for me to accept something like this?”

“I bought it for you, Hyunsung-ssi.”

As though trying to make up all at once for the time he hadn’t been able to look after his companions, Kim Dokja bought armfuls of things from the [Dokkaebi Bag] every day, dressing his companions in the clothes or using the other items on them. The children were especially excited.

“Hey, look at this, Shin Yoosung!”

“I got one too, you know?”

Draped from head to toe in all kinds of accessories Kim Dokja had bought them, Shin Yoosung and Lee Gilyoung roamed the streets with silly grins. Jung Heewon chuckled at the sight.

“He’s turned the kids into Christmas trees.”

The two squabbling children were sitting on Lee Hyunsung’s broad shoulders. Lee Hyunsung was grinning just as widely. He seemed quite taken with the new shield Kim Dokja had given him.

“……Three idiots.”

Muttering that, she glanced to the side and saw Lee Jihye approaching with a beaming smile, wearing a helmet shaped like a triangular gimbap.

If those three were Christmas trees, this one was a three-tier cake.

“Hasn’t Dokja ajusshi been really nice to us lately?”

“Maybe he’s finally realized how precious his companions are.”

At Jung Heewon’s response, Lee Jihye narrowed her eyes and said, “Hmm.”

“Unnie…… Don’t tell me you didn’t get anything?”

“I don’t need anything, okay?”

In fact, Kim Dokja had visited Jung Heewon several times too.

But there had been no items, only information about a few hidden pieces and instruction in training methods.

She could still vividly recall Kim Dokja’s oddly dejected face as he left after she retorted that she could manage on her own now without him telling her those things.

She was about to rap the snickering Lee Jihye on the head when someone poked her shoulder.

Turning around, she found Kim Dokja, who looked rather tired.

“Oh, Dokja-ssi……”

With deep dark circles under his eyes, Kim Dokja held something out to Jung Heewon.

“This is……”

“A new suit. You’ll find it easier to move in.”

Jung Heewon accepted the clothes before she quite knew what she was doing.

It was a blue-black commando uniform with a large cape attached. She had seen it at the exchange once, but had given up on it because it was too expensive.

“This is far too expensive. What I have now is more than enough……”

Kim Dokja silently shook his head. Looking at his unreadable expression brought back an old memory. Come to think of it, he had given her clothes like this back in their Chungmuro days too.

They’d been rags back then……

[The constellation ‘Bald General of Justice’ feels hurt by the incarnation ‘Jung Heewon.’]

“You promised to be my sword, didn’t you? This is the least I can do.”

Leaving her with those words, Kim Dokja was already far away, apparently with something else to do. Watching his retreating back, Jung Heewon stroked the commando uniform left in her hands. Beside her, Lee Jihye teased,

“Unnie, your mouth……”

“What?”

“Nothing, there’s just something at the corners of your mouth. Anyway, unnie, if you don’t like it, trade it for my admiral’s helmet. I’ve always dreamed of wearing a commando uniform.”

“No.”

Looking closely, she found an emblem on the uniform similar to the one on Lee Hyunsung’s shield. Mah-deh bah-ee…… Yahn-guh…… saen……? Not being fluent in English, Jung Heewon scratched her head and gave up trying to read it. Anyway, it had to be good.

“But why is that guy suddenly acting like this? I heard he even charged people for food……”

“Who knows. Maybe he’s cooking up another weird scheme, like before.”

It certainly wouldn’t be strange for Kim Dokja.

Having given them such good items, he would surely work them to the bone again.

Feeling like an office worker who had received an advance on her salary, Jung Heewon tried draping the uniform’s coat this way and that. As she and Lee Jihye discussed how to wear it to look even cooler, someone appeared like a ghost and trudged past them.

“Sangah-ssi, is something wrong?”

“Pardon? Oh, yes. No.”

Yoo Sangah, who had been staring blankly into space, started and opened and closed her mouth. Empty eyes. Realizing something was wrong, Jung Heewon was about to speak, but Lee Jihye beat her to it.

“Aha, I get it. You didn’t get an item either, Sangah unnie?”

Jung Heewon jabbed her in the side, and Lee Jihye doubled over with a small squeal. Yoo Sangah smiled weakly.

“I suppose I’ve just had a lot on my mind lately. ……Anyway, that outfit looks wonderful, Heewon-ssi.”

“Oh, yes. Dokja-ssi gave it to me…… Though now that I’ve tried it on, I’m wondering if it’s a bit much.”

“I think it suits you.”

“You do? Thank you.”

Embarrassed, Jung Heewon scratched her head.

A bracelet she hadn’t seen before gleamed on Yoo Sangah’s wrist.

Just as things were about to get awkward, Jung Heewon saw an opening.

“By the way, how are things with Dokja-ssi these days?”

“……Dokja-ssi?”

Yoo Sangah’s face as she repeated the question, as though she had no idea what Jung Heewon meant.

Wondering if she had gotten the wrong idea, Jung Heewon stumbled over her words.

“Um, uh, I mean…… I was just wondering if you two were getting along……”

Tilting her head, Yoo Sangah touched her lips and murmured,

“Hmm, I think it’s about the same as when we worked at the company……”

About the same as when they worked at the company. Judging by that response, the prospects didn’t look very promising. Lee Jihye whispered to Jung Heewon.

“I told you before. There’s nothing between them. Dokja ajusshi’s tastes don’t run that way, they run toward our……”

“……Now I see why my sponsor likes you. Has that precious master of yours woken up?”

“Not yet. They said it would take a few more days.”

Far away, Kim Dokja was limply making his way toward the ward like a squid, unaware that they were talking about him.

“Maybe he’s going to give him something too……”

The sun was slowly sinking beyond the nearly completed clock tower. The group’s boisterous laughter rang out. Gong Pildu could be seen taking apart something like a machine gun, while Han Myungoh tested his new prosthetic leg in various ways.

For some reason, Jung Heewon’s heart swelled.

They were all together now.

Soon they would return to Earth and plunge once more into the hell of the scenarios. Even so, Jung Heewon wasn’t afraid. Watching the sun set behind the clock tower, she recalled something she had heard in the Theatre Dungeon.

―I wished to see the epilogue of a certain novel.

Kim Dokja had looked so lonely when he said that.

Back then, she hadn’t even understood what he meant, but now she felt she might understand a little. And there was at least one thing she knew for certain.

When that ‘epilogue’ came, Kim Dokja would not be alone.

Several days after the banquet. The constellations staying in the [Industrial Complex] began to leave one by one. I was about to go outside to see them off when I found two flowers tucked into my coat’s breast pocket.

“What are these?”

A pair: a red cosmos and a lily.

Quite a mismatched combination. ……Was this the kids’ doing?

For now, I took the flowers with me and went out to the square. Several constellations were already passing through the portal. Some of those leaving had signed special contracts with the nebula <Kim Dokja’s Company>, and the old man in front of me was one of them.

[You look like a man preparing for war. There should be no need to rush quite so much.]

“Scenarios are always war.”

The ‘Mass Production Maker’ laughed heartily at my reply.

[Don’t grow numb to it. I hope you’ll become someone different from the other constellations.]

“Thank you for your help.”

I smiled as well and bowed my head. Opening the door of his vehicle, the ‘Mass Production Maker’ paused and looked back at me.

[There’s one last thing I’d like to ask……]

“Of course. Ask anything you like.”

But the Mass Production Maker didn’t ask his question right away.

Instead, he took a cigarette from inside his clothes and worked it between his lips.

[……Have you ever thought about what ■■ actually is?]

The lighter clicked, and the tip of the cigarette caught fire.

Exhaling smoke like a sigh, the Mass Production Maker continued.

[Is it somewhere we reach because we want to, or somewhere fate makes us reach? Is it a place, a life, or perhaps a particular time and space?]

The ‘Mass Production Maker’ had probably turned those questions over countless times.

And in the end, he probably hadn’t found an answer.

“What’s certain is that it’s where every story ends.”

[Sometimes I find your composure quite remarkable.]

“I get nervous too.”

[I’ve thought this since the Gourmet Association, but you’re worse at lying than I expected.]

The Mass Production Maker smiled like a child.

[Which is why I ask…… Is your ■■ really the ‘Final Chapter’?]

A question that caught me off guard.

I reflexively parted my lips, then closed them again.

The ‘Mass Production Maker’ waited patiently for my answer. I only spoke after the cigarette in his hand had burned down by about half.

“I don’t know where the story will take me. But…… I’m certain that the Final Chapter is where I want to go.”

Even after I finished speaking, he continued to listen.

As though my story were still going on.

Only after the remaining half of his cigarette had burned away did the ‘Mass Production Maker’ smile.

[I hope I’m on your last page too.]

“Have a safe journey, sir.”

[Take care of yourself.]

With a soft hum of its engine, the ‘Mass Production Maker’s’ vehicle slid into the portal. The other constellations disappeared through it one by one. As the portal closed, ominous sparks writhed on the far side of the empty sky.

Watching those sparks, I gently stroked the flower petals in my pocket.

About three days remained.

Soon, this [Industrial Complex]’s most important story would begin.

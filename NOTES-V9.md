# External-reference annotation pass — version 9

The margin explains independently lookable-up language, history, mythology, natural history, and other external references. It is not an in-book encyclopedia. Purely fictional identities, materials, ranks, and plot help do not qualify. A coined word may qualify through a documented external root; the note explains that root. Possible derivations or namesakes begin with “Possibly” in both the margin and full note. Definitions say what the external thing is without discussing the author’s intentions.

This pass removes purely contextual entries, rewrites mixed notes, and researches missed proper nouns against dictionaries, classical texts, historical reference works, church traditions, and heritage collections. Sources support the external facts, not a proven identity for a fictional person. Unsubstantiated candidates remain unannotated.
